Launch sequence

This checklist uses live school data. Complete the operational steps in order, but computer repairs do not have to delay programme preparation.

Quick actions

1. Student roster

Import the school roster and approve student activations.

 2. Teacher / facilitator accounts

Approve facilitators before assigning them to programmes.

 3. Programme management

Create, edit and assign flexible school programmes.

 4. Parent access

Approve parents and verify parent-child links.

 5. Reports

Confirm sessions, attendance, assignments and submissions appear correctly.

Pilot starter programmes

These are editable launch templates, not fixed clubs. Keep, rename, remove or replace any item to match the school.

Existing school programmes should be strengthened, not replaced. The Mental Health & Wellness template is intentionally privacy-safe for parent access.

3-account pilot test

Before opening the system to the full school, use one approved school admin, one approved facilitator and one approved student.

Online pathway

Admin assigns facilitator → student joins/is assigned → session recorded → attendance marked → assignment issued → student submits → facilitator reviews → school report updates.

Offline / paper pathway

Student completes work offline → facilitator records the submission on the student’s behalf → school and approved parent see the appropriate progress status.

On-site ICT check

The portal work can continue before the hardware visit. On site, verify the number of functional systems, internet access, browser login/logout, session-only authentication, projector/power availability and which machines need repair or replacement.

Shared school computers should keep “Private device only” OFF. Students should log out after each session; inactive shared sessions are automatically protected by the portal timeout.
