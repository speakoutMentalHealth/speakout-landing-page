Editorial verification checklist

For every submitted review, confirm the exact resource version, curriculum evidence, independent checks, safeguarding, licensing, and the reviewer’s qualifications before any separate server-side approval process. This dashboard does not publish approval decisions.

1. Evidence

Verify official curriculum source, edition, learning outcomes and original references.

2. Independent verification

Recheck accuracy, answer keys, child suitability, accessibility and permissions.

3. Sign-off and audit

Record reviewer identity, independent editor, resource version or hash, decision, timestamp and next review date in an authorised system.
