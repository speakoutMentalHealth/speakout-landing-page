Your volunteer journey starts here

This is your starting point for training, assessment, programme information and communication with SpeakOut. You do not need to wait for individual instructions to begin your free learning pathway.

What happens next?

  1. Account approval

    Your SpeakOut account has been approved for access to this volunteer hub. This is not yet approval to work independently with beneficiaries.

  2. Complete your free volunteer learning

    Read the five foundation lessons on safe listening, recognising risk, boundaries, privacy and cultural sensitivity.

    Open volunteer training
  3. Take the qualifying assessment

    After the lessons, follow the official assessment link inside the training centre. Keep your certificate reference for subsequent verification.

  4. Safeguarding and role review

    SpeakOut will check your suitability for the specific role, confirm boundaries, supervision, location and availability before assigning activities.

  5. Begin approved assignments

    Once the team confirms your placement, use official email and WhatsApp communication to receive assignment details and reporting instructions.
