Primary 1 · English Studies · Guided sample lesson

Read, Understand and Tell a Story

Learn new words, read a short story about Ada, answer questions and explain what you learned.

Development lesson, awaiting facilitator and curriculum review. This is original SpeakOut sample content. Inspire, Ignite and eLearn resources have not yet been imported or independently verified for this lesson. No learner information is saved.

Step 1 · What you will learn

Our learning goals

  • Read a short passage with help when needed.
  • Identify the people, objects and events in a story.
  • Answer questions using information in the passage.
  • Retell the story in your own words.

Words to know: bag, pencil, book, friend, break time, share.

Step 2 · Read or listen

Ada and Her Red Bag

Ask a parent or facilitator to read aloud with you, or take turns reading each sentence.

Ada has a red bag. Every morning, she takes her bag to school. Inside the bag are a book, a pencil and a small bottle of water. Ada likes to read her book. Her friend Bisi sits beside her in class. At break time, Ada shares a story with Bisi. After school, Ada puts her book back in her bag and walks home with her mother.

Step 3 · Think and discuss

Let's understand the story

  1. Point to the sentence that tells you the colour of Ada's bag.
  2. Find the names of the two children in the story.
  3. Tell a friend what Ada did after school.

Facilitator guidance: Ask the child to show the sentence that supports each answer. Explain unfamiliar words, model reading aloud and allow spoken responses.

Step 4 · Practise

Answer the questions

When you are ready, open English practice. Choose Primary 1 and English Studies. Read the passage again while answering the 12 sample questions.

Start English practice →

Step 5 · Show what you know

Tell your own short story

Draw your school bag or describe it aloud. Name three things you put inside it. Tell someone why one of those things is useful.

Success check: I can name the characters, find facts in the passage and retell what happened.
