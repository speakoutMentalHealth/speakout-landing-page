Primary 1–6 · English Studies · Guided sample lessons

Read, Understand and Practise

Read a story, learn key vocabulary, talk about what happened and then answer comprehension questions.

Draft learning materials: Original SpeakOut sample stories, not yet educator-approved or verified against the official curriculum. Specific partner assets and rights clearance are pending. No learner information is saved.

Step 1 · Learning goals

What you will learn

  • Read a short story with support when needed.
  • Identify characters, events and important details.
  • Explain answers using sentences from the story.
  • Retell the main events in your own words.

Step 2 · Read

Read aloud with a teacher or family member. Pause after each paragraph to discuss unfamiliar words.

Step 3 · Talk about it

Find evidence in the story

  1. Who is the story mainly about?
  2. Where does the story take place?
  3. What happens first, next and last?
  4. Which sentence helps you answer a question about the story?

Facilitator guidance: Invite learners to point to evidence in the passage. Accept spoken responses, model unfamiliar vocabulary and avoid rushing.

Step 4 · Practise

Answer comprehension questions

Keep the story open as you work. You can return to it whenever you need to check a detail.

Start English practice →

Step 5 · Reflect

Retell and create

Tell a partner the story in three sentences. Draw one event from the story or write two sentences about what you learned.

Success check: I can explain who, where, what happened and how I know.
