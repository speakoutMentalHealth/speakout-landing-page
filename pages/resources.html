Start Here

Simple guidance for students, parents, teachers and schools.

These public resources educate visitors and help them know the next safe step.

🚨

Immediate Danger

If someone may harm themselves or another person, contact local emergency services or a trusted adult immediately.

💬

Talk to Someone

Do not carry emotional pressure alone. Speak with a trusted adult, counselor or health professional.

🧠

Know the Signs

Withdrawal, hopelessness, sudden mood changes, self-harm talk, bullying and extreme stress need attention.

🔐

Use the Portal

Approved members can access deeper resources, learning materials and role-specific guidance privately.

Students

For young people

You are not weak for needing support. The first step is to speak safely.

  • Name what you feel
  • Talk to a trusted person
  • Avoid isolation
  • Seek help early
Parents

For families

Look for changes in behavior and respond with patience, not shame.

  • Listen before correcting
  • Avoid threats and insults
  • Watch digital pressure
  • Work with school support
Teachers

For educators

A supportive teacher can become a bridge between a struggling student and help.

  • Notice behavior changes
  • Document concerns
  • Refer appropriately
  • Protect student dignity
