Academy FAQ

Common questions from parents, schools and learners.

Can children join without prior coding experience?

Yes. Beginner pathways are designed for learners with no prior experience.

Can parents book home classes?

Yes. SpeakHub can support private or small-group home learning requests where available.

Is the academy only for children?

No. SpeakHub supports children, teenagers, adults, parents and teachers.

Are certificates available?

Certificates are available for selected structured courses, bootcamps and workshops.

Can schools enroll students?

Yes. Schools can request academy programs as part of school partnership packages.

Can lessons be online only?

Yes. Some classes can be online, while others may include practical sessions.
