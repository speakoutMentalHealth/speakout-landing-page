Privacy & Cookies

This notice explains how SpeakOut Mental Health Outreach handles personal information, learning activity, cookies, advertising technologies and third-party services on speakoutmentalhealth.org.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Information we may process

Depending on the feature you use, SpeakOut may process account information such as your name, email address, role, school or organization details, profile information, course or reading progress, certificate records, form submissions and support requests.

Our website and service providers may also receive technical information such as browser type, device information, IP-derived location, security logs, page requests and cookie or similar identifier data.

How we use information

We use information to operate accounts and learning services, provide requested content, track progress, issue certificates, respond to enquiries, maintain security, prevent abuse, improve the service and meet legal or policy obligations.

Advertising and Google AdSense

SpeakOut uses Google AdSense on selected learning and media pages, including course players, book readers and SpeakOut TV or audio experiences. The rest of the website is not intended to be covered with advertising.

Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and protect advertising. Depending on applicable law and your settings, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

SpeakOut may use Google's Offerwall or rewarded-ad experience on selected content pages. Where available, visitors may be offered an advertising experience in exchange for temporary access to content. Google controls whether an eligible rewarded ad is available and the experience is subject to Google's own policies and user choices.

The AdSense integration is not designed to pass your SpeakOut profile fields, learning progress, certificate records or private form responses to advertisers as ad-targeting fields.

Cookies and consent

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for sign-in, security, remembering preferences, service functionality, embedded media and advertising. Where consent is required, we use the relevant consent or messaging controls made available by our service providers.

You can also manage advertising preferences through your browser and Google account settings.

Third-party services

SpeakOut uses third-party platforms to provide parts of the service, including Google/Firebase infrastructure and embedded services such as YouTube and Spotify. Those providers may process information under their own privacy terms when you use their features.

Young people and wellbeing information

SpeakOut serves young people, schools, families and communities. Users should avoid entering private medical or highly sensitive personal information into public forms, comments or advertising interactions. Advertising components are kept separate from protected learning records and account data.

Data choices and contact

You may contact SpeakOut to ask questions about this notice or about information you have provided through our services. Use our contact page or email speaaakout@gmail.com.

This notice may be updated as the website, advertising configuration or legal requirements change. The latest version will remain available at this URL.
