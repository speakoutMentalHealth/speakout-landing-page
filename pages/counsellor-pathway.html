Professional standards matter.

Training and examination are free. Completing an online course or receiving a completion certificate does not grant a counselling licence or automatically approve someone for a counselling role.

  • Eligibility and credentials review
  • Safeguarding and suitability checks
  • Role-specific approval before practice
How the pathway works

Four clear stages.

Each stage builds toward safe, accountable involvement in SpeakOut programmes.

01

Check eligibility

Review professional qualifications, relevant experience and the requirements of the role you intend to pursue.

02

Complete training and assessment

Use the existing MindCheck learning and examination pathway. Existing training records and certificates remain in place.

Open learning pathway →
03

Submit credentials for review

Follow the application and verification instructions. SpeakOut must assess identity, credentials, safeguarding and suitability.

04

Receive role-specific guidance

Only applicants who complete the required review may be considered for an appropriate role. Training completion alone does not authorize practice.
