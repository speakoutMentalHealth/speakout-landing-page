WHY ON THE MOVE

Support should reach people where they already are.

SpeakOut works with institutions and communities to deliver structured, culturally responsive wellbeing programs, practical tools and appropriate pathways to further support.

PROGRAMS

Choose the pathway your community needs.

School Wellness

Mental-health literacy, resilience, healthy coping, help-seeking and student wellbeing.

Campus Wellness

Support for academic pressure, adjustment, relationships, resilience and student life.

Faith & Mental Wellness

Compassionate mental-health education designed to work respectfully within faith communities.

Workplace Wellness

Practical wellbeing education, healthier workplace culture and stress-management support.

Community Wellness

Accessible outreach for NGOs, youth groups, associations and underserved communities.

Parents & Educators

Tools that help adults communicate, support young people and recognize when more help is needed.

HOW IT WORKS

One request. A structured pathway.

01

Apply

Tell us about your institution, audience and needs.

02

Review

Our team reviews need, safety, feasibility, logistics and available support.

03

Plan

If approved, we agree the program, date, responsibilities and logistics.

04

Activate

Once confirmed, SpeakOut delivers the agreed program with appropriate facilitators.

05

Continue

Where appropriate, we gather feedback and identify useful follow-up, resources or longer-term support.

BEFORE YOU APPLY

What to have ready.

Your organization details, primary contact, intended audience, estimated number of participants, the need you want the program to address, venue/logistics information and your current funding position. Preferred dates are helpful but do not constitute a booking.

WHO CAN APPLY

Schools, campuses, faith organizations, NGOs, workplaces, youth groups, community organizations and public institutions.

Submitting a request does not guarantee approval. Programs may be sponsored, SpeakOut-supported, co-funded or institution-funded depending on availability, suitability and need.
