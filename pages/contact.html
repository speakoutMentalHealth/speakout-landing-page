Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for visitors.

Can schools partner with SpeakOut?

Yes. Schools can request awareness programs, school mental health clubs, teacher support and parent engagement.

Can parents register?

Yes. Parents can apply for access to parent resources and support pathways.

Can professionals volunteer?

Yes. Mental health professionals, educators, media professionals and community leaders can support outreach and training.

How can donors support?

Donors can sponsor school clubs, workshops, training, digital access, community campaigns and media education.
