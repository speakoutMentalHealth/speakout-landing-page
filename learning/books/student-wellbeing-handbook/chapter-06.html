Core Lesson

Worry is a normal part of life. Anxiety becomes a problem when fear feels overwhelming, constant, or stops you from doing everyday things.

Anxiety can show up as racing thoughts, stomach discomfort, sweating, shaking, avoiding situations, trouble sleeping, or feeling restless.

Grounding techniques help bring your attention back to the present. One simple method is the 5-4-3-2-1 technique: name five things you see, four you feel, three you hear, two you smell, and one you taste.

If anxiety is affecting your schoolwork, sleep, relationships, or safety, speak with a trusted adult, counsellor, health professional, or support service.
