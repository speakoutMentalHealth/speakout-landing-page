Learning Objectives

  • Understand the main idea of this chapter.
  • Reflect on your own wellbeing experience.

Core Lesson

Your body and mind are connected. Sleep, food, movement, hydration, and health habits influence mood, concentration, and resilience.

Poor sleep can increase irritability, anxiety, forgetfulness, and low motivation. Students need consistent rest to learn and function well.

Movement does not have to be intense. Walking, stretching, dancing, sports, or simple exercise can improve mood and reduce stress.

Small daily habits often matter more than dramatic changes. A healthier routine can begin with water, sleep, movement, and regular meals.

Reflection Questions

  1. How many hours do you sleep on most nights?
  2. What physical habit affects your mood?
  3. What small health habit can you improve?
