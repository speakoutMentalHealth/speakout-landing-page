Learning Objectives

  • Understand the main idea of this chapter.
  • Reflect on your own wellbeing experience.
  • Identify one practical action you can apply.

Core Lesson

Resilience is the ability to recover, adapt, and continue growing after challenges. It does not mean nothing hurts. It means you keep building strength.

Setbacks can teach us, but they should not define us. Failing an exam, losing a friendship, or making a mistake does not make your life a failure.

Resilient students use support, learn from experience, manage emotions, adjust plans, and keep hope alive.

Resilience grows through small habits: rest, reflection, problem-solving, gratitude, healthy relationships, and courage to try again.

Reflection Questions

  1. Describe a setback you survived.
  2. What helped you recover?
  3. What lesson did you learn from that experience?

Practical Activity

Complete a short reflection in your workbook. Write one action you will practice before the next lesson.

Knowledge Check

  1. What is one key idea from this chapter?
  2. Name one practical action you can take this week.
  3. True or False: Wellbeing grows through small consistent actions.

Key Takeaways

  • Wellbeing is practical and can be strengthened.
  • Small steps matter.
  • Support is part of growth.
