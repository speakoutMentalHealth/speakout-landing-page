Core Lesson

Welcome to the SpeakOut Student Wellbeing Handbook. This handbook is your companion for understanding yourself, strengthening your wellbeing, and becoming a positive influence in your school and community.

Student wellbeing is the overall state of your emotional, mental, social, and physical health while you are learning. It affects how you think, feel, relate to others, respond to stress, and make decisions.

The SpeakOut approach is built on four pillars: mental wellbeing, emotional wellbeing, social wellbeing, and personal growth. These pillars work together to help students thrive in school and life.

This handbook is not a medical textbook or a replacement for professional help. It is a practical learning resource that encourages self-awareness, healthy habits, reflection, and responsible help-seeking.

Reflection Questions

  1. What does wellbeing mean to you?
  2. Which area of your wellbeing needs the most attention right now?
  3. Who are three trusted people you can talk to?

Practical Activity

Knowledge Check

  3. True or False: Wellbeing grows through small consistent actions.

Key Takeaways

  • Wellbeing is practical and can be strengthened.
  • Small steps matter.
  • Support is part of growth.
