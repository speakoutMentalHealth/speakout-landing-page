Learning Objectives

  • Identify one effective study technique.
  • Recognize early signs of burnout.
  • Practice realistic weekly planning.

Core Lesson

Academic success is important, but it should not destroy your health. Balance helps you learn sustainably.

Effective study habits include planning ahead, reviewing regularly, asking questions, taking breaks, sleeping well, and avoiding last-minute panic.

Burnout can happen when you push yourself for too long without rest. Signs include exhaustion, loss of motivation, irritability, poor concentration, and feeling hopeless.

Productivity is not only doing more. It is doing what matters with focus, planning, and recovery.

Reflection Questions

  1. What study habit works best for you?
  2. What is one sign you may need rest?
  3. How can you plan your week better?

Practical Activity

Write a realistic plan for one week that includes study time, rest and one activity you enjoy.

Knowledge Check

  1. Name one study technique described in this chapter as more effective than re-reading notes.
  2. What is burnout?
  3. True or False: Balance means never working hard.

Key Takeaways

  • Effective studying depends more on method than total hours spent.
  • Burnout is exhaustion from prolonged pressure without recovery.
  • Rest and connection deserve a genuine place in your weekly plan.
