Result Release Policy

For safety, academic results are managed by approved school administrators only. Configure the default release rule and whether an upload may publish immediately.

PIN delivery is manual: the school gives the generated PIN directly to the authorized parent/guardian. PINs are not sent automatically by the platform.

Release Workflow

Safe result access

1. Upload and review the result.
2. Confirm school-fee clearance.
3. Generate the PIN. The plaintext PIN is shown only once.
4. Publish the result. Parents with an approved child link can unlock and download it.

PINs are stored only as hashes. Revoking or regenerating a PIN invalidates previous parent unlocks.
