    How External Learning Works

    1. Open the course on the official provider website.
    2. Complete the course directly with that provider.
    3. Return to SpeakHub after completion.
    4. Upload your certificate or accepted proof of completion.
    5. SpeakHub reviews the submission and records the approved external achievement.
