Not yet open: Uploads, paid course sales and payouts are not currently enabled through this programme.

How review and publication will work

  1. Educators apply and their qualifications are checked.
  2. Editors assign appropriate subjects and resources.
  3. Reviewers submit evidence-based findings and recommendations.
  4. An independent authorized editor verifies the review and approves any publication.
  5. Reviewers who opt in receive accurate public credit on accepted resources.
  6. Course creators will be onboarded only after licensing, payment and safeguarding policies are operational.

Integrity and safeguarding: Reviewers cannot approve their own courses. Payment never guarantees a favorable review. No draft learning resources are certified by this programme, and no earnings are guaranteed. Applications and uploads will open only after secure review and consent systems are ready.
