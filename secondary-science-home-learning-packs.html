Secondary science learning packs

Printable, low-equipment science activities for JSS 1–3 and SS 1–3. All six packs are unreviewed drafts.

Educator review pending. These supplementary drafts have not been mapped against an authoritative Nigerian curriculum edition or validated for examination preparation. Activities are limited to observation, drawing, reading and discussion. No laboratory chemicals, electrical experiments, handling of blood/body samples, or unsupervised fieldwork. A qualified science teacher must verify scientific accuracy and suitable progression before use in a school.
