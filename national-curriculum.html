Tertiary programmes

There is no single tertiary school syllabus. University degrees use applicable NUC programme standards; technical qualifications use NBTE curricula; NCE programmes use NCCE standards. Professional qualifications also require their regulator. Programme, qualification and institution requirements must be identified before authoring materials.
