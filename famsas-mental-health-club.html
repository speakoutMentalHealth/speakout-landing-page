About the Club

More than awareness. A campus wellbeing movement.

The FAMSAS Mental Health Club gives students access to mental health education, peer support, e-library materials, workshops, skills-building activities and referral guidance through the SpeakOut ecosystem.

📚

E-Library Access

Curated mental health, leadership, academic and personal development resources.

🧠

Mental Health Education

Simple learning materials that help students understand stress, emotions and help-seeking.

🤝

Peer Support

A safe community that encourages students to speak, connect and support each other.

💼

Skills Acquisition

Life skills, leadership and personal growth activities connected to student development.

🎓

Workshops & Certificates

Seminars, campaigns, trainings and recognition for completed club activities.

🛡️

Safe Support Pathway

Confidential guidance and referral direction when students need additional help.
