Clean Up E-Library Books

Finds book documents with no real content (no reading content, no purchase link, no download link) left over from earlier seeding, previews the exact list, then deletes only those on confirmation. Real books - including any using a downloadUrl, purchaseUrl, bookSlides, or content field - are never touched.
