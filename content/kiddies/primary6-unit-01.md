# Primary 6 Unit 1: Evidence, Fractions and Problem Solving

## About this unit
Original SpeakOut introductory Primary 6 English Studies and Mathematics pack. Class placement is a proposed teaching level, not verified alignment to individual Nigerian NERDC class outcomes. No teacher approval or supervised trial is claimed. Earlier classes remain available for revision, and the material does not constitute a complete term.

## Facilitator preparation and inclusion
Prepare safe paper drawings and a notebook. Read text aloud if needed, explain unfamiliar vocabulary in a learner's familiar language, and accept speech, signing, pointing, dictation or other communication aids. Provide breaks, adapt the quantities and do not require personal disclosures. Check understanding with a new example after modelling. Suggested lesson length is 15–25 minutes, adapted to needs.

## English Lesson 1: Separate fact and inference

Aim: practise separate fact and inference using an original worked example.
Model: The passage says the road is wet. It does not state why. Explain the key concept carefully and demonstrate it on paper.
Together: ask the learner to explain or point to the clue in the example. Revisit prerequisite skills where needed and model the answer once without pressure.
Try: Does a wet road prove it rained? Ask the learner to show how they know, not only tap a correct choice. Then invite a fresh example using new names, numbers or drawings.
Check and answer: No. Rain is possible, but the cause was not given. Record whether help was needed.
Next step: repeat with a different short example, checking the reason behind the learner's response.
