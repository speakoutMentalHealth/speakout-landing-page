# Curriculum evidence intake and deferred school mapping

The partner-school schemes of work are **not yet available**. This is not a blocker to drafting safe provisional teaching resources, but **is a blocker to claiming alignment, approval or readiness for classroom publication**.

## Evidence intake fields (to complete when documents arrive)

| Evidence field | Required value | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| School and authorised curriculum contact | School name, role, permission to use scheme | Awaiting school |
| Class and term | Primary level, term, session/year | Awaiting school |
| Subject label and optional subjects | Exact school naming and selections | Awaiting school |
| Scheme edition | Document title, issue date and version | Awaiting school |
| Weekly topics | Topic, week, time allocation, learning expectations | Awaiting school |
| NERDC source | Official curriculum edition, class-specific outcome text/ID/page | Unverified |
| Mapping reviewer | Qualified teacher, subject/class, dated signoff | Not received |
| Learner pilot | Anonymised observations, consent/safeguarding process, revisions | Not conducted |

## Reconciliation procedure

1. Keep original uploaded school scheme intact. Record provenance, edition and permitted usage.
2. Map each week/topic to an existing draft unit and record **matched / partial / missing / conflicting**; do not silently relabel an unmatched unit as aligned.
3. Check exact official NERDC subject/class outcome edition and page. Leave unmapped where inaccessible.
4. Draft missing lessons, revise mismatched ones and document every content change with a versioned PR.
5. Obtain independent qualified-teacher dated signoff per class, subject and version, followed by supervised learner pilot evidence.
6. Only publish approved versioned units through the existing release gate. Never overwrite student records or bypass access controls.

## Work possible before documents arrive

Develop provisional sequenced lesson drafts, offline worksheets, age-appropriate formative checks, accessibility and privacy-safe automated tests. These may be previewed as **development-only** materials, not certified school schemes.
