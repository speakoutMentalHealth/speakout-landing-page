# Primary 1 — Term 1 english studies foundation sequence (draft)

**Release status:** Original provisional teaching material. Four sequenced units, not a complete term or an approved NERDC-mapped scheme. School scheme reconciliation, teacher review and learner pilot are pending.

**Suggested pacing:** One unit per teaching week, adaptable to learner needs; pacing is not an official school timetable. Use local examples without requiring personal data.

## Unit 1: Sounds and initial letters

**Observable objective:** By the end, the learner can answer the focus question and demonstrate the idea with objects, drawing, speech or writing.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Ask what learners already know about sounds and initial letters; show a familiar safe object, picture or short written example.

**Teacher modelling (10 minutes):** Model /m/ gently, then invite the learner to identify it. Think aloud through one example before asking pupils to practise independently.

**Guided practice (10 minutes):** Which word begins with the /m/ sound: mat or sun? Invite different strategies, including speaking, pointing and writing.

**Independent worksheet (10 minutes):** Draw a mat and a sun; underline the word that begins with m. Include space for a learner drawing or spoken response recorded by the facilitator.

**Answer / success criterion:** mat. Accept equivalent accurate explanations; check both the answer and reasoning.

**Misconception to check:** Confusing letter names with sounds.

**Exit question:** Which word begins with the /m/ sound: mat or sun? A learner should explain or demonstrate the answer without public ranking.

**Home/offline alternative:** Use paper drawings or safe household objects; participation must not depend on internet access or paid materials.

**Accessibility and safeguarding:** Large-print copies, oral or non-writing responses, clear instructions, no timed scoring, no identifying learner data. Supervise all physical activities and respect individual ability.

## Unit 4: Sequencing a story

**Observable objective:** By the end, the learner can answer the focus question and demonstrate the idea with objects, drawing, speech or writing.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Ask what learners already know about sequencing a story; show a familiar safe object, picture or short written example.

**Teacher modelling (10 minutes):** Use three picture cards and rearrange them together. Think aloud through one example before asking pupils to practise independently.

**Guided practice (10 minutes):** First Bisi plants a seed. Next she waters it. Later a leaf grows. What happens after planting? Invite different strategies, including speaking, pointing and writing.

**Independent worksheet (10 minutes):** Draw three panels for first, next and later; label or dictate each. Include space for a learner drawing or spoken response recorded by the facilitator.

**Answer / success criterion:** She waters it.. Accept equivalent accurate explanations; check both the answer and reasoning.

**Misconception to check:** Confusing event order.

**Exit question:** First Bisi plants a seed. Next she waters it. Later a leaf grows. What happens after planting? A learner should explain or demonstrate the answer without public ranking.
