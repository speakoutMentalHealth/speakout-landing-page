## Unit 9: Decimal place value

**Learning aim:** Demonstrate the concept by explaining a worked example and solving a related problem.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Recall a related earlier skill. Invite verbal, drawn or pointed responses.

**Teacher demonstration (10 minutes):** Seven tenths is 0.7. Represent the steps visually, checking understanding before proceeding.

**Guided question (10 minutes):** What is the value of 7 in 12.75?

**Worked response:** 0.7. Seven tenths is 0.7.

**Independent / supported practice (10 minutes):** Write 3 + 0.4 + 0.08 as one decimal.

**Expected answer:** 3.48. Accept mathematically valid alternative working.

**Reteaching:** Ask which step caused difficulty, model a simpler numerical example with paper or counters, and then repeat the question with support.

**Exit check:** Ask learners to explain one step and how to check their answer. Record anonymous patterns only.

**Accessibility and safety:** Offer extra time, large-print questions, home-language explanation, nonverbal responses and seating options. No rankings, identifying child data or mandatory purchases.

**Release gate:** Review curriculum outcome/page, answer accuracy, age suitability, approved teacher/date and supervised pilot. All remain unverified.
