# Primary 3 term-one provisional weeks 5–8 — cultural creative arts

**Scope:** Four new original extension units after weeks 1–4; not a complete term, verified NERDC alignment, school scheme or approved timetable.

## Week 5: Repeated motifs

**Learning outcome:** Explain and apply the idea through writing, speech, drawing or pointing.

**Connect (5 min):** Recall one earlier skill with a safe fictional example.

**Teacher modelling (10 min):** Demonstrate a worked example and narrate the steps; use local paper materials where possible.

**Guided assessment (10 min):** What is a motif in a pattern? Invite reasoning and allow thinking time.

**Printable worksheet (10 min):** Create a border using one repeated leaf motif.

**Marking guidance:** A repeated design element. Two criteria: accurate result and justified reasoning. Accept equivalent oral, drawn and written responses.

**Misconception and reteaching:** Every shape in a pattern must differ. Show a counterexample and permit a retry.

**Exit check (5 min):** Ask an analogous question and note only anonymised misconceptions for lesson improvement.

**Accessibility and safety:** Large print, visual support, read-aloud, oral/pointing/drawing and seated alternatives. Avoid timed ranking, personal data collection and hazardous activities; supervise practical work.

**Evidence gate:** NERDC outcome/page **unverified**; school scheme **awaiting school**; teacher approval **not received**; supervised pilot **not conducted**.
