## Week 1: Hygiene

**Foundation question:** When should you wash your hands before eating?

**Expected answer:** Before touching food.

**Marking:** 1 point for accurate or safe answer; 1 for an appropriate explanation or demonstration. Accept oral, drawn and pointing responses.

**Application question:** Which is safer after using a toilet: washing hands with soap and water or wiping on clothes?

**Expected answer:** Wash with soap and water.

**Marking:** 1 point for accurate or safe answer; 1 for an appropriate explanation or demonstration. Accept oral, drawn and pointing responses.

**Transfer question:** A pupil has dirty hands before lunch. What should they do?

**Expected answer:** Wash with soap and water before eating.

**Marking:** 1 point for accurate or safe answer; 1 for an appropriate explanation or demonstration. Accept oral, drawn and pointing responses.
