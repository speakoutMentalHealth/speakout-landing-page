## Unit 9: Distinguishing fact and opinion

**Learning aim:** Read, discuss and apply distinguishing fact and opinion in a clear sentence or short response.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Recall a related skill from earlier units. Accept an oral, written or pointed response.

**Original classroom text/example:** Our class library has twenty books. The library is the best room in the school.

**Teacher demonstration (10 minutes):** Read the example aloud, model the target skill and explain one choice in plain language.

**Guided question (10 minutes):** Which sentence is an opinion?

**Expected guided response:** The library is the best room in the school.

**Independent or supported practice (10 minutes):** Write one verifiable fact and one opinion about a school garden.

**Answer/review guidance:** Clearly distinguish an observable claim from a personal view. Accept accurate alternatives and give constructive private feedback.

**Reteach and exit check:** Model a shorter example, ask the pupil to try again, then invite one sentence explaining the key skill.

**Accessibility and safeguarding:** Offer home-language explanation, large print, read-aloud, dictation, drawing or pointing. Avoid personal disclosures, identifying data, public rankings and timed penalties.

**Release gate:** Qualified teacher must check originality, grammar, child suitability and mapping to the applicable Nigerian curriculum outcome; review date and supervised pilot not yet supplied. Keep as draft.

## Unit 12: Organising a short report

**Learning aim:** Read, discuss and apply organising a short report in a clear sentence or short response.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Recall a related skill from earlier units. Accept an oral, written or pointed response.

**Original classroom text/example:** The school planted five trees. Pupils watered them weekly. Four survived the dry month.

**Teacher demonstration (10 minutes):** Read the example aloud, model the target skill and explain one choice in plain language.

**Guided question (10 minutes):** What is the report mainly about?

**Expected guided response:** The school's tree planting and care.

**Independent or supported practice (10 minutes):** Write a three-sentence report about a class clean-up, separating what happened from opinion.

**Answer/review guidance:** A factual sequence with subject, action and result. Accept accurate alternatives and give constructive private feedback.
