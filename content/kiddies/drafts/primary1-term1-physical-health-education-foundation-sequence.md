**Status:** Four provisional, original developmental units. Not a complete term, verified NERDC mapping or teacher-approved school scheme. School scheme integration deferred until documents arrive.

## Unit 1: Safe movement spaces

**Observable objective:** Learners can demonstrate the main idea using speech, drawing, pointing or writing.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Invite a familiar example of safe movement spaces; use a picture or teacher-provided object, never personal or sensitive disclosures.

**Teacher modelling (10 minutes):** Model checking for obstacles before activity.

**Guided practice (10 minutes):** Before moving in class, what should you check? Allow thinking time and discuss more than one sensible response.

**Independent worksheet (10 minutes):** Draw a safe play area; circle obstacles to avoid.

**Answer / success criterion:** That there is enough clear, safe space. Equivalent accurate, respectful responses are acceptable.

**Misconception to check:** Moving quickly in crowded areas.

**Exit question:** Before moving in class, what should you check? Invite the learner to explain the reason, without timed scoring or public ranking.

**Offline and accessible routes:** Large print, read-aloud instructions, paper drawing, oral responses, pointing, keyboard-friendly digital entry and adapted/seated movement as appropriate. Supervise movement and do not require paid materials.

## Unit 4: Working together safely

**Observable objective:** Learners can demonstrate the main idea using speech, drawing, pointing or writing.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Invite a familiar example of working together safely; use a picture or teacher-provided object, never personal or sensitive disclosures.

**Teacher modelling (10 minutes):** Use a verbal scenario, not a physical fall demonstration.

**Guided practice (10 minutes):** A friend falls during a game. What should you do? Allow thinking time and discuss more than one sensible response.

**Independent worksheet (10 minutes):** Draw a safe helping action; complete: 'I can help by ___'.

**Misconception to check:** Continuing a game while someone needs help.
