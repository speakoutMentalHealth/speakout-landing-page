## Unit 9: Comparing sources

**Learning aim:** Read, discuss and apply comparing sources in a clear sentence or short response.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Recall a related skill from earlier units. Accept an oral, written or pointed response.

**Original classroom text/example:** Notice A says the event starts at 9 a.m. Notice B says it starts at 10 a.m.

**Teacher demonstration (10 minutes):** Read the example aloud, model the target skill and explain one choice in plain language.

**Guided question (10 minutes):** What information differs?

**Expected guided response:** The announced starting time.

**Independent or supported practice (10 minutes):** Write one question to ask the organiser before sharing the event.

**Answer/review guidance:** What is the confirmed starting time? Accept accurate alternatives and give constructive private feedback.

**Reteach and exit check:** Model a shorter example, ask the pupil to try again, then invite one sentence explaining the key skill.

**Accessibility and safeguarding:** Offer home-language explanation, large print, read-aloud, dictation, drawing or pointing. Avoid personal disclosures, identifying data, public rankings and timed penalties.

**Release gate:** Qualified teacher must check originality, grammar, child suitability and mapping to the applicable Nigerian curriculum outcome; review date and supervised pilot not yet supplied. Keep as draft.
