# Primary assessment content audit — 2026-10-11

**Status:** Engineering/editorial audit, not a qualified teacher's curriculum review. No claim of NERDC alignment, classroom readiness or pilot success.

## Verified examples of material issues

- **Primary 4 mathematics week 6 mismatch:** existing provisional lesson covers **mixed numbers**, while the tiered week 6 assessment bank covers **fraction simplification**. These are related but not interchangeable objectives; both require an evidence-backed week map before publication.
- **Primary 4 mathematics week 5:** assessment bank includes fragment '54 books on 6 shelves' with answer '9 per shelf'. The intended question is inferable but must explicitly ask how many books per shelf.
- **Primary 1–2 English PR #234:** initial version contained single-character questions and answer keys. The open branch was corrected; this illustrates why structural counts do not establish content validity.
- **Five-subject weeks 1–4 scaffolds:** generic instructions to invent a fictional classroom example are **not complete questions**. These files must not be labelled classroom-ready or treated as 360 validated items.
- **Safety and science:** check context-sensitive answers against age and local safeguarding guidance, not merely the presence of an answer line.

## Remediation order

1. Verify each grade/subject/week against the actual existing lesson objective; record any mismatch and propose a teacher-approved correction rather than silently renaming content.
2. Replace fragments and underspecified scenarios with full interrogative or imperative prompts, relevant source passages and unique expected responses.
3. Independently verify numerical computations, scientific claims, factual wording and safety instructions. Record reviewer's name, qualifications and date when available.
4. Include full-credit, partial-credit and incorrect examples; verify that transfer items genuinely change context or require a new inference.
5. Conduct accessibility review, NERDC source/page verification, school-scheme reconciliation, teacher approval and supervised pilot before catalogue publication.
