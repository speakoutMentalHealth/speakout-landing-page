## Unit 9: Living and non-living things

**Objective:** Explain the concept through observation, discussion and a simple example.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Recall a related lesson using a familiar object, picture or teacher drawing.

**Teacher model (10 minutes):** A mango tree grows and needs water; a stone does not grow. Explain the example clearly and invite learners to describe what they notice.

**Guided question (10 minutes):** Which is living, the tree or stone?

**Answer guidance:** Tree.

**Independent or supported task (10 minutes):** Name another living thing and one non-living thing.

**Review guidance:** Examples: goat and cup. Allow accurate equivalent responses.

**Exit ticket:** Give one real-world example of the concept and describe why it fits. Reteach with an easier illustration where necessary.

**Inclusion and safeguarding:** Permit pointing, dictation, drawings, seated participation and home-language scaffolding. Use adult-supervised, low-risk materials, and do not collect identifying learner information or rank pupils.
