## Week 5: Soil types

**Learning outcome:** Explain and apply the idea through writing, speech, drawing or pointing.

**Connect (5 min):** Recall one earlier skill with a safe fictional example.

**Teacher modelling (10 min):** Demonstrate a worked example and narrate the steps; use local paper materials where possible.

**Guided assessment (10 min):** Which soil often feels gritty: sandy or clay? Invite reasoning and allow thinking time.

**Printable worksheet (10 min):** Match illustrated soil samples to descriptions.

**Marking guidance:** sandy soil. Two criteria: accurate result and justified reasoning. Accept equivalent oral, drawn and written responses.

**Misconception and reteaching:** All soil absorbs water equally. Show a counterexample and permit a retry.

**Exit check (5 min):** Ask an analogous question and note only anonymised misconceptions for lesson improvement.

**Accessibility and safety:** Large print, visual support, read-aloud, oral/pointing/drawing and seated alternatives. Avoid timed ranking, personal data collection and hazardous activities; supervise practical work.
