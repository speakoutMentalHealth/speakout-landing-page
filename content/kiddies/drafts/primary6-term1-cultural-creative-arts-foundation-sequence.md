# Primary 6 Term 1 — cultural creative arts provisional foundation sequence

**Status:** Four original sequenced units, not a complete term or verified NERDC/school-scheme mapping. Qualified-teacher approval and supervised pilot remain pending.

## Unit 1: Visual hierarchy

**Learning objective:** Learner explains the key concept and demonstrates it through writing, speech, pointing or drawing.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Introduce visual hierarchy using a safe, familiar fictional example; do not ask for sensitive disclosures.

**Teacher modelling (10 minutes):** Compare layouts with clear headings.

**Guided practice (10 minutes):** What should stand out first on a school event poster? Invite multiple approaches and explain the reasoning.

**Independent worksheet (10 minutes):** Create a readable poster with title, date and location placeholders.

**Answer / marking guidance:** The main message or event title. Accept accurate equivalents and reasoning, not just memorised answers.

**Misconception check:** Every text item must be the same size.

**Exit assessment:** Use the focus question or a comparable scenario; record anonymised teaching observations only, not student identifiers or public rankings.

**Accessibility and safety:** Provide large-print, read-aloud, oral, pointing, drawing and seated alternatives; no timed tests, forced movement, personal-data collection or unsafe materials. Supervise practical work.

**Evidence gate:** Official NERDC edition/outcome/page **unverified**; partner-school scheme **awaiting school**; teacher sign-off **not received**; supervised pilot **not conducted**.

## Unit 4: Creative revision

**Learning objective:** Learner explains the key concept and demonstrates it through writing, speech, pointing or drawing.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Introduce creative revision using a safe, familiar fictional example; do not ask for sensitive disclosures.

**Teacher modelling (10 minutes):** Discuss thoughtful choices and artistic intent.

**Guided practice (10 minutes):** How can feedback improve a draft artwork? Invite multiple approaches and explain the reasoning.

**Independent worksheet (10 minutes):** Sketch a before-and-after design and describe one change.

**Answer / marking guidance:** Revise a specific element while keeping the goal. Accept accurate equivalents and reasoning, not just memorised answers.

**Misconception check:** All feedback must be accepted.
