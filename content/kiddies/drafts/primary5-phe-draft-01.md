# Primary 5 Physical & Health Education — Responsible Choices

> **Editorial draft — not published to learners.** Original SpeakOut content awaiting independent teacher review. Detailed NERDC edition, term, page and outcome alignment: **unverified**. No learner pilot or school sign-off is claimed.

## Facilitator safeguards and access

All situations are fictional. Learners may speak, sign, point, type, write or dictate, and may opt out of personal discussion. Offer large print, plain-language summaries, extra time and offline paper equivalents. Never require touching, strenuous activity, medical disclosure, recording, or public ranking. Follow school safeguarding policies. Seek qualified medical assistance for concerning symptoms; this lesson is not diagnosis or treatment advice.

## Lesson 1: Recognising exertion and rest

**Goal:** Evaluate a fictional choice, give a reason and identify a safe alternative.

**Scenario:** During an optional school activity, a learner feels dizzy.

**Facilitator explanation:** Stop the activity, tell a supervising adult and seek appropriate help; never insist on finishing.

**Offline guided activity:** Make a paper decision tree with stop, tell and support steps.

**Interactive practice (oral, paper or digital prompt):** What is the first safe response to dizziness during activity? Allow response selection and explanation; offer neutral retry feedback without collecting identifying information.

**Answer and reasoning:** Stop, alert a supervising adult and follow their safety guidance. Discuss alternatives respectfully.

**Adaptation:** Offer picture cards, text-to-speech or a dictated answer; no physical demonstration is needed.

## Lesson 2: Personal hygiene and shared equipment

**Goal:** Evaluate a fictional choice, give a reason and identify a safe alternative.

**Scenario:** A sports group has only one personal towel.

**Facilitator explanation:** Do not share personal hygiene items; ask an adult for a suitable alternative.

**Offline guided activity:** Sort fictional items into personal and commonly shared categories, explaining exceptions.

**Interactive practice (oral, paper or digital prompt):** Should learners share a personal towel? Why? Allow response selection and explanation; offer neutral retry feedback without collecting identifying information.

**Answer and reasoning:** No; use individual clean items and seek adult support. Discuss alternatives respectfully.

**Adaptation:** Offer picture cards, text-to-speech or a dictated answer; no physical demonstration is needed.

## Lesson 3: Accessible teamwork

**Goal:** Evaluate a fictional choice, give a reason and identify a safe alternative.

**Scenario:** A classmate wants to contribute without running.

**Facilitator explanation:** Offer meaningful roles such as scoring, pointing, planning or seated play without singling anyone out.

**Offline guided activity:** Write two inclusive game rules and a respectful invitation.

**Interactive practice (oral, paper or digital prompt):** How can everyone contribute to a team activity? Allow response selection and explanation; offer neutral retry feedback without collecting identifying information.

**Answer and reasoning:** Offer different safe roles and ask classmates what works for them. Discuss alternatives respectfully.

**Adaptation:** Offer picture cards, text-to-speech or a dictated answer; no physical demonstration is needed.
