# Primary 4 Basic Science and Technology — six original developmental lessons

**Status:** Unapproved draft preview. Not a complete term or verified NERDC class-specific outcome map. Activities require appropriate adult supervision; no hazardous experiments.

## Lesson 1 — Living things

**Learning intention:** Observe and explain living things at a Primary 4 level.

**Facilitator demonstration:** Use pictures, ordinary classroom objects or verbal descriptions. Ask the learner to predict, observe and explain; do not use electricity, chemicals, sharp tools or unknown substances.

**Interactive question:** Name one way to care for a living plant

**Answer key:** Water it appropriately.

**Printable worksheet:** (A) Name one way to care for a living plant (B) Explain a cause and an effect using two sentences. (C) Draw or describe one safe real-life example.

**Assessment guidance:** Accept an accurate response plus a relevant explanation or representation; ask a follow-up question to check understanding.

**Accessibility:** Provide audio reading, large-print paper, alternative text descriptions, spoken answers and non-timed participation.
