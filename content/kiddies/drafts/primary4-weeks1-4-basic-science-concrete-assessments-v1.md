**Provisional draft:** 12 original prompts with model answers. These week slots are NOT verified against school schemes or NERDC. No hazardous activity or mains electrical work.

## Week 1: Food chains

**Foundation question:** Which is a producer: green grass or a goat?

**Expected answer:** Green grass.

**Marking:** 1 for scientifically accurate answer and 1 for relevant explanation or safe reasoning; accept oral, drawn or written responses.

**Application question:** In grass → goat → human, what eats the grass?

**Expected answer:** The goat.

**Marking:** 1 for scientifically accurate answer and 1 for relevant explanation or safe reasoning; accept oral, drawn or written responses.

**Transfer question:** If grass becomes scarce, what might happen to goats?

**Expected answer:** They may have less food.

**Marking:** 1 for scientifically accurate answer and 1 for relevant explanation or safe reasoning; accept oral, drawn or written responses.

**Reteach:** Demonstrate with a safe image or familiar observation, then ask a different question.

**Progress check:** Use a fresh fictional scenario and anonymised feedback only.
