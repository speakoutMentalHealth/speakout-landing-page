## Unit 1: Read for meaning

**Learning objective:** Learner demonstrates the focus skill by speaking, pointing, writing or drawing.

**Starter (5 min):** Ask a familiar, non-sensitive question about read for meaning and show a safe picture or example.

**Teacher modelling (10 min):** Track each word and reread for evidence.

**Guided practice (10 min):** Read: 'Kemi carried two books to class.' How many books? Invite learners to explain their thinking.

**Independent worksheet (10 min):** Underline the words that tell how many; draw the scene.

**Answer / marking guidance:** Two books. Accept equivalent accurate explanations and ask for reasoning.

**Misconception check:** Guessing without reading the sentence.

**Exit check:** Repeat the focus question in a fresh example; note anonymised learning gaps for lesson revision, not public ranking.

**Accessibility and safeguarding:** Offer large-print, oral, pointing, drawing and seated alternatives. No timer, names, stored learner scores, unsafe movement or required purchases. Adult supervision for practical activities.
