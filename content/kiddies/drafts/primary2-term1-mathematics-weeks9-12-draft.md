## Provisional Unit 9: Two-digit number patterns

**Learning goal:** Demonstrate the two-digit number patterns skill using a written, spoken, pointed or drawn response.

**Connect (5 min):** Revisit one relevant earlier skill using familiar school materials. Ask learners to explain rather than race.

**Teacher demonstration (10 min):** Model the key idea step by step. Count in fives from 85.

**Guided question (10 min):** Fill 85, 90, 95, __, __.

**Guided response and reasoning:** 100, 105. Count in fives from 85.

**Independent / supported practice (10 min):** Write two more numbers after 115 when counting by fives.

**Practice answer:** 120, 125. Accept equivalent accurate strategies and accessible response formats.

**Misconception check and reteach:** Ask the learner to show each step using number cards, counters, drawings or a number line as appropriate. If the answer differs, check the representation of the quantities before retrying with smaller numbers.

**Exit ticket:** Invite a brief explanation of why the answer makes sense, or ask for a related example. Note only anonymous teaching needs.

**Safeguarding and accessibility:** Adult oversight; no paid materials, identifying pupil data, public rankings or timed penalties. Offer home-language scaffolding, large print and seated/oral alternatives.

**Publication gate:** Outcome ID, official curriculum edition, teacher reviewer/date and classroom pilot remain **unverified**. Do not activate or certify this draft.
