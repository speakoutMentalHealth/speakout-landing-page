## Unit 1: Warm and cool colours

**Learning objective:** Learner demonstrates the focus skill by speaking, pointing, writing or drawing.

**Starter (5 min):** Ask a familiar, non-sensitive question about warm and cool colours and show a safe picture or example.

**Teacher modelling (10 min):** Show examples and invite creative interpretation.

**Guided practice (10 min):** Which colour is usually called warm: orange or blue? Invite learners to explain their thinking.

**Independent worksheet (10 min):** Draw two small patterns, one using warm colours and one cool.

**Answer / marking guidance:** orange. Accept equivalent accurate explanations and ask for reasoning.

**Misconception check:** Treating colour groups as strict rules.

**Exit check:** Repeat the focus question in a fresh example; note anonymised learning gaps for lesson revision, not public ranking.

**Accessibility and safeguarding:** Offer large-print, oral, pointing, drawing and seated alternatives. No timer, names, stored learner scores, unsafe movement or required purchases. Adult supervision for practical activities.
