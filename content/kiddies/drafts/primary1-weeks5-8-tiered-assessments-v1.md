# Primary 1 — tiered weekly assessment checks (provisional weeks 5–8)

**Status:** Original formative sample items for six subjects, four weekly topics per subject. Week labels are placeholders pending partner-school schemes and verified NERDC outcome/page mapping; not a complete assessment programme.

**Scoring:** Each question earns 1 point for correct response and 1 point for an age-appropriate explanation, drawing, worked steps or relevant evidence (6 points per topic). Teacher uses private, non-diagnostic feedback. After an error, model a parallel example and retry with a new question; do not collect learner identifiers.
