# Primary 1 — provisional term-one weeks 5–8: social citizenship

**Status:** Original extension of four foundation units (weeks 1–4). This provides eight provisional units across the subject, not a complete school-approved term. Week numbers are planning placeholders, not verified NERDC or school pacing.

## Week 5: Classroom responsibilities

**Objective:** Learner demonstrates the concept through speech, writing, pointing or drawing.

**Connect to earlier units (5 min):** Recall a relevant week 1–4 idea and invite a safe familiar example.

**Model (10 min):** Work through a simple teacher-created example with pictures, counters or gestures, explaining each step.

**Guided practice (10 min):** What can pupils do to help keep desks tidy? Ask learners to show how they know.

**Independent worksheet (10 min):** Draw a before-and-after tidy desk.

**Expected response and marking:** Put materials away safely. Accept equivalent correct explanations and adapt for spoken or drawn responses.

**Misconception to check:** Only the teacher cares for the classroom. Re-model with a second example if needed.

**Exit assessment (5 min):** Ask the focus question with altered details; provide immediate, private formative feedback.

**Differentiation and accessibility:** Large print, clear language, visual cues, oral responses, pointing and paper alternatives; no timers, public rankings or learner identifiers. Supervise practical tasks.
