# Primary 2 Term 1 — social citizenship provisional foundation sequence

**Status:** Four original sequenced foundation units; not a complete term or verified NERDC/school-scheme mapping. Teacher sign-off and learner pilot remain pending.

## Unit 1: Community roles

**Learning objective:** Learner demonstrates the focus skill by speaking, pointing, writing or drawing.

**Starter (5 min):** Ask a familiar, non-sensitive question about community roles and show a safe picture or example.

**Teacher modelling (10 min):** Discuss cleaners, teachers and school support staff.

**Guided practice (10 min):** Who can help children learn in school? Invite learners to explain their thinking.

**Independent worksheet (10 min):** Draw a community helper and label their contribution.

**Answer / marking guidance:** Teacher. Accept equivalent accurate explanations and ask for reasoning.

**Misconception check:** Assuming only one person supports a school.

**Exit check:** Repeat the focus question in a fresh example; note anonymised learning gaps for lesson revision, not public ranking.

**Accessibility and safeguarding:** Offer large-print, oral, pointing, drawing and seated alternatives. No timer, names, stored learner scores, unsafe movement or required purchases. Adult supervision for practical activities.
