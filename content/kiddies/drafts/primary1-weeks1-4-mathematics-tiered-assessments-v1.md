# Primary 1 mathematics — provisional weeks 1–4 tiered assessment and teacher answer guide

**Scope:** Four draft topic slots; not official NERDC outcome mapping, complete term content or a validated exam. Teachers must reconcile sequencing against school schemes before delivery.

**Formative rubric:** Each item 1 mark for correct answer, 1 mark for demonstrated method, representation or justification. Total 6 per topic. Allow oral, written, drawn and manipulatives-based responses. No public ranking or collection of pupil identifiers.

## Week 1: Counting and order

**Teacher objective:** Assess understanding and transfer of counting and order with concrete and symbolic representations.

**Foundation item:** Count 7 objects.

**Answer:** 7

**Marking:** 1 for answer, 1 for relevant working, explanation or drawing; give method credit for sensible reasoning even if an arithmetic slip occurs.

**Application item:** What comes after 19?

**Answer:** 20

**Marking:** 1 for answer, 1 for relevant working, explanation or drawing; give method credit for sensible reasoning even if an arithmetic slip occurs.

**Transfer item:** A basket has 8 mangoes and receives 2 more. Count the new total.

**Answer:** 10

**Marking:** 1 for answer, 1 for relevant working, explanation or drawing; give method credit for sensible reasoning even if an arithmetic slip occurs.

**Reteach and misconception check:** Model counting and order with a number line, counters, place-value chart or labelled drawing as appropriate. Ask learner to explain a corrected example before an unseen parallel item.

**Progress:** Check an unfamiliar but comparable task after feedback; note only anonymised group patterns and reteaching needs.
