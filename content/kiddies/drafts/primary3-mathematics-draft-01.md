# Primary 3 Mathematics — original introductory draft

**Status:** Unapproved development preview; three sample lessons, not a complete scheme. Detailed NERDC outcome mapping, teacher review and learner pilot remain pending.

## Lesson 1

**Learning aim:** Explain and practise the skill in the question.

**Facilitator guide:** Introduce the scenario, model a worked example, ask the learner to explain their reasoning, and provide supportive feedback.

**Interactive practice prompt:** Four groups of six mangoes total how many?

**Answer key:** 24.

**Printable worksheet:** Copy the question, draw or write the steps, and create one similar example with its answer.

**Worksheet answer guidance:** Compare the learner's result with the answer key and check the explanation, not just the final answer.

**Accessibility:** Allow spoken, typed, large-print, paper and manipulative responses. Do not require speed, personal information or student accounts.
