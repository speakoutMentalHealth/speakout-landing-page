# Primary 6 English Studies — extended reading and communication draft

**Status:** Six original developmental lessons, not a complete term, verified NERDC outcome mapping or teacher-approved material. This draft does not replace existing units.

**Learning sequence:** Reading comprehension, vocabulary, sentence construction, evidence, composition and oral communication. A facilitator should adapt vocabulary and support to the learner's actual reading level.

## Lesson 1 — Read for meaning

**Learning aim:** Practise read for meaning using an original age-adjusted passage.

**Reading passage:** The debating club proposed a reading corner for the school. Some pupils worried about the cost, while others suggested donated books and recycled shelves. The class agreed to begin with a small trial and evaluate how often the corner was used.

**Interactive practice:** Describe one concern and one proposed solution.

**Expected response / marking guidance:** Concern: cost; solution: donated books or recycled shelves. Accept equivalent responses when the explanation is accurate.

**Printable worksheet:** (1) Read or listen to the passage. (2) Describe one concern and one proposed solution. (3) Underline a useful word or phrase, or dictate it to an adult. (4) Draw a picture or write one sentence showing your understanding.

**Facilitator notes:** Read aloud if needed, model one example, invite a learner explanation, and give feedback without public comparison or timed ranking.

**Accessibility:** Offer oral response, large print, keyboard entry, paper response and adult-supported reading; do not require personal information.
