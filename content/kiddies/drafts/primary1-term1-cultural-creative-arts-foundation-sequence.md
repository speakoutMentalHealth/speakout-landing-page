**Status:** Four provisional, original developmental units. Not a complete term, verified NERDC mapping or teacher-approved school scheme. School scheme integration deferred until documents arrive.

## Unit 1: Lines and shapes

**Observable objective:** Learners can demonstrate the main idea using speech, drawing, pointing or writing.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Invite a familiar example of lines and shapes; use a picture or teacher-provided object, never personal or sensitive disclosures.

**Teacher modelling (10 minutes):** Trace shapes slowly and count their sides.

**Guided practice (10 minutes):** Which shape has three sides? Allow thinking time and discuss more than one sensible response.

**Independent worksheet (10 minutes):** Draw a triangle, square and circle; decorate each with a different line.

**Answer / success criterion:** A triangle. Equivalent accurate, respectful responses are acceptable.

**Misconception to check:** Confusing a square and a rectangle.

**Exit question:** Which shape has three sides? Invite the learner to explain the reason, without timed scoring or public ranking.

**Offline and accessible routes:** Large print, read-aloud instructions, paper drawing, oral responses, pointing, keyboard-friendly digital entry and adapted/seated movement as appropriate. Supervise movement and do not require paid materials.

## Unit 4: Tell a picture story

**Observable objective:** Learners can demonstrate the main idea using speech, drawing, pointing or writing.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Invite a familiar example of tell a picture story; use a picture or teacher-provided object, never personal or sensitive disclosures.

**Teacher modelling (10 minutes):** Reorder three illustrated cards together.

**Guided practice (10 minutes):** A child finds a seed, plants it and sees a leaf. What happens in the middle? Allow thinking time and discuss more than one sensible response.

**Independent worksheet (10 minutes):** Draw three picture panels labelled beginning, middle and end.

**Answer / success criterion:** The child plants the seed. Equivalent accurate, respectful responses are acceptable.

**Misconception to check:** Giving events out of order.

**Exit question:** A child finds a seed, plants it and sees a leaf. What happens in the middle? Invite the learner to explain the reason, without timed scoring or public ranking.
