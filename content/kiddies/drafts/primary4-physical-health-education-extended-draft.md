# Primary 4 physical health education — extended draft

**Status:** Six original developmental lesson outlines; not a complete term, not verified NERDC outcome mapping, not teacher-approved. These are inclusive, low-risk classroom activities; adapt to learner needs and school policies.

## Lesson 1 — Warm-up

**Learning aim:** Describe and apply warm-up in a familiar situation.

**Facilitator guidance:** Model the activity with words, images or safe objects; invite thoughtful, respectful responses. Offer seated or adapted movement, avoid competition and stop any activity causing discomfort.

**Interactive question:** Why should we warm up gently before activity?

**Answer guidance:** To prepare the body for movement. Accept equivalent sensible explanations.

**Printable worksheet:** (A) Why should we warm up gently before activity? (B) Give one reason and one example. (C) Write, draw or dictate a real-life example and discuss it with a facilitator.

**Assessment:** Check explanation and practical understanding, not speed or public ranking.

**Accessibility:** Large print, oral and written responses, keyboard-friendly digital prompts, no timed scoring, and appropriate physical adaptations.
