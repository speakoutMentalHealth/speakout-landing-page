## Week 5: Design balance

**Outcome:** Explain and apply the focus skill using an accessible response mode.

**Connect (5 min):** Revisit a relevant weeks 1–4 idea using a safe fictional example.

**Teacher modelling (10 min):** Work through a comparable example aloud with paper, visuals or safe local materials.

**Guided assessment (10 min):** What is visual balance in a poster? Ask for evidence or calculation steps.

**Printable worksheet (10 min):** Sketch two layouts and mark the clearer one.

**Answer / two-criterion rubric:** A considered distribution of elements. Criterion A: accurate response; criterion B: clear supporting reasoning. Accept oral, written, pointing or drawn equivalents.

**Misconception / reteach:** Symmetry is the only kind of balance. Demonstrate a counterexample and offer a retry.

**Exit assessment (5 min):** Use an analogous scenario and give private feedback; record anonymised teaching patterns only.

**Accessibility and safeguarding:** Large print, read-aloud, visuals, oral/drawn responses and seated alternatives. No timed public rankings, learner personal data, unsupervised movement or hazardous materials.

**Evidence gate:** Official NERDC outcome/page **unverified**; school scheme **awaiting school**; qualified-teacher approval **not received**; supervised pilot **not conducted**.
