## Unit 9: Sequence and time words

**Learning aim:** Read, discuss and apply sequence and time words in a clear sentence or short response.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Recall a related skill from earlier units. Accept an oral, written or pointed response.

**Original classroom text/example:** Zara planted seeds on Monday. She watered them on Tuesday. On Friday, small leaves appeared.

**Teacher demonstration (10 minutes):** Read the example aloud, model the target skill and explain one choice in plain language.

**Guided question (10 minutes):** Which event happened last?

**Expected guided response:** Small leaves appeared on Friday.

**Independent or supported practice (10 minutes):** Retell the events using first, next and finally.

**Answer/review guidance:** Seeds planted, watered, then leaves appeared. Accept accurate alternatives and give constructive private feedback.

**Reteach and exit check:** Model a shorter example, ask the pupil to try again, then invite one sentence explaining the key skill.

**Accessibility and safeguarding:** Offer home-language explanation, large print, read-aloud, dictation, drawing or pointing. Avoid personal disclosures, identifying data, public rankings and timed penalties.
