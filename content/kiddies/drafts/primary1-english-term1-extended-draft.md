# Primary 1 English Studies — Term 1 extended original draft

**Editorial status:** Development draft only. Six original sample lessons; NOT a complete term or verified NERDC outcome map. Not approved by a qualified teacher or tested with learners.

**Access and safeguarding:** No child names, scores, audio recordings or identifiers are collected. All activities work on paper or orally. The named characters are fictional.

## Lesson 1 — Listen and retell

**Intended skill:** Listen and retell. Read, listen to or examine the original passage and explain an answer using its wording.

**Original reading / listening text:** Ada has a red bag. She puts a book in it. She walks to class.

**Facilitator sequence:** Read aloud slowly; point to each sentence. Invite individual thinking, paired discussion and non-competitive feedback. Model one response before independent practice.

**Interactive practice prompt:** What did Ada put in her bag?

**Answer / evidence:** A book.

**Printable worksheet:** (1) What did Ada put in her bag? (2) Draw Ada's bag and label the book; say what happens first and next. (3) Highlight or dictate a word or sentence from the passage that supports your response.

**Marking guidance:** Credit the stated answer and other evidence-based equivalents; give feedback on reasoning, not reading speed. For open-ended writing, check clarity, relevant details and appropriate punctuation rather than requiring identical wording.

**Accessibility and extension:** Offer large print, spoken response, reading partner, pointing, typing or handwriting. Learners may use a picture or sentence frame; confident learners explain a second interpretation supported by the text.

## Lesson 6 — Describe a picture

**Intended skill:** Describe a picture. Read, listen to or examine the original passage and explain an answer using its wording.

**Original reading / listening text:** A small yellow bird sits on a tall tree.

**Facilitator sequence:** Ask learners to identify nouns and describing words using a drawing. Invite individual thinking, paired discussion and non-competitive feedback. Model one response before independent practice.

**Interactive practice prompt:** Name two words that describe the bird.

**Answer / evidence:** Small and yellow.

**Printable worksheet:** (1) Name two words that describe the bird. (2) Draw the bird and tree; write or dictate 'The bird is ...' with two describing words. (3) Highlight or dictate a word or sentence from the passage that supports your response.

**Marking guidance:** Credit the stated answer and other evidence-based equivalents; give feedback on reasoning, not reading speed. For open-ended writing, check clarity, relevant details and appropriate punctuation rather than requiring identical wording.
