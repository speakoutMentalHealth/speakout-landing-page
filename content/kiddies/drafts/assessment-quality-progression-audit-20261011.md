# Curriculum assessment quality and progression review — initial audit

**Scope:** Primary 1–6 draft assessment banks. This is a preliminary editorial audit, not qualified-teacher sign-off or official NERDC verification.

## Material risks and required corrections

1. **Lesson-to-assessment mismatch:** Provisional weekly headings can differ between lesson drafts and tiered banks (e.g., Primary 4 mathematics week 6 lesson: mixed numbers; week 6 assessment: equivalent fractions). Before use, build a one-to-one mapping of grade, subject, week, lesson objective, assessment item and answer key. Resolve differences with the actual school scheme rather than relabelling silently.
2. **Generic non-assessable prompts:** The five-subject weeks 1–4 scaffolds refer to fictional examples without supplying a full stimulus. These are authoring briefs, not classroom-ready worksheets; replace them with concrete age-appropriate scenarios, expected answers, plausible misconceptions and teacher-marked exemplars.
3. **Question-level marking validity:** 'One mark for reasoning' must be tied to a specific worked method or observable explanation. Provide sample full-credit, partial-credit and incorrect responses per item before summative use.
4. **Progression:** Verify a developmental ladder from concrete identification (Primary 1–2) to comparison and explanation (Primary 3–4) and justified multi-step transfer (Primary 5–6), while accommodating different learning needs.
5. **Factual accuracy:** Independently recalculate all numeracy keys and review science, safeguarding and health statements with qualified subject experts. Automated heading-count tests do not validate correctness.
6. **Curriculum evidence:** Obtain dated NERDC source documents, exact outcome/page references and school schemes, then log teacher reviewer, decision, date and revisions. Do not claim official alignment from provisional topics.
7. **Assessment fairness:** Provide large-print, oral, visual, drawing, accessible movement and alternative response routes; do not require disclosure of personal safety incidents or pupil data.
8. **Pilot:** Run supervised teacher-led trials, document anonymised item ambiguity, difficulty and accessibility issues, and revise before student catalogue release.

## Release gate checklist

- [ ] School scheme received and week-to-outcome map reconciled
- [ ] NERDC outcome/page evidence verified
- [ ] Every item has a complete stimulus and unambiguous model answer or rubric
- [ ] Answer keys and grade-level progression independently reviewed
- [ ] Accessibility and child-safeguarding reviewed
- [ ] Qualified teachers sign off on revisions
- [ ] Supervised pilot and anonymised feedback completed
- [ ] Catalogue publishing approved separately
