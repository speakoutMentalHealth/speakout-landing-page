## Unit 1: Energy transformation

**Learning objective:** Learner explains the key concept and demonstrates it through writing, speech, pointing or drawing.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Introduce energy transformation using a safe, familiar fictional example; do not ask for sensitive disclosures.

**Teacher modelling (10 minutes):** Discuss transfers without dismantling devices.

**Guided practice (10 minutes):** What energy change occurs in a battery-powered torch? Invite multiple approaches and explain the reasoning.

**Independent worksheet (10 minutes):** Draw an energy-flow diagram.

**Answer / marking guidance:** Chemical energy to electrical energy to light and heat. Accept accurate equivalents and reasoning, not just memorised answers.

**Misconception check:** Energy is created from nothing.

**Exit assessment:** Use the focus question or a comparable scenario; record anonymised teaching observations only, not student identifiers or public rankings.

**Accessibility and safety:** Provide large-print, read-aloud, oral, pointing, drawing and seated alternatives; no timed tests, forced movement, personal-data collection or unsafe materials. Supervise practical work.
