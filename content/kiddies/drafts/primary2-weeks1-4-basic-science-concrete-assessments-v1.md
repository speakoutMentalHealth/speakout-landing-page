## Week 1: Hand hygiene

**Foundation question:** Name one time to wash hands.

**Expected answer:** Before eating or after toilet use.

**Marking:** 1 point for accurate or safe answer; 1 for an appropriate explanation or demonstration. Accept oral, drawn and pointing responses.

**Application question:** Why is soap useful when washing hands?

**Expected answer:** It helps remove dirt and germs.

**Marking:** 1 point for accurate or safe answer; 1 for an appropriate explanation or demonstration. Accept oral, drawn and pointing responses.

**Transfer question:** After playing outside, a pupil wants to eat. What should they do first?

**Expected answer:** Wash hands with soap and water.

**Marking:** 1 point for accurate or safe answer; 1 for an appropriate explanation or demonstration. Accept oral, drawn and pointing responses.

**Reteach:** Show a safe everyday example, explain the key idea and check a different example.
