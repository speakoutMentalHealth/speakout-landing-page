# Primary 1 Mathematics — Term 1 extended original draft

**Status:** Six distinct developmental lesson outlines; NOT a complete approved term, a verified NERDC outcome map, or a substitute for teacher lesson plans. School calendar, official edition and human review pending.

**Facilitator approach:** Begin with locally available, safe manipulatives and an accessible demonstration. Invite a learner explanation before correction. Do not collect student names, marks or other personal data in this draft.

## Lesson 1 — Counting and grouping

**Learning intention:** Demonstrate counting and grouping with a concrete example and explain the result.

**Explore:** Show the situation with drawings, counters, a number line or written notation appropriate to Primary 1. Ask learners to predict, explain, and check.

**Interactive practice question:** Count 12 bottle tops; make groups of 5 and 2

**Expected response:** 12 tops; 2 groups of 5 and 2 left.

**Printable worksheet (independent):** (A) Solve: Count 12 bottle tops; make groups of 5 and 2 (B) Draw a diagram or write a second method. (C) Create a similar question, exchange it with a partner and check each other's explanation.

**Answer and marking guidance:** A: 12 tops; 2 groups of 5 and 2 left. For B/C, check that the representation, operation and reasoning agree; accept equivalent valid strategies.

**Support and extension:** Provide large-print or oral prompts and physical objects; challenge confident learners to change one quantity and explain how the answer changes. Never grade by speed.
