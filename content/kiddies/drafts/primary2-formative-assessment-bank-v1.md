# Primary 2 provisional formative assessment bank — six subjects

**Draft only.** These are sample checks, not a complete topic-by-topic bank, psychometrically validated examination, official NERDC alignment or teacher-approved assessment. No learner names, public leaderboards or saved scores.

**Marking per item:** 1 point for correct response; 1 point for a clear explanation, representation or relevant evidence. Maximum 6 per subject. Use oral, written, drawn or pointing responses. Treat 0–2 as needing guided reteaching, 3–4 as developing, 5–6 as ready for a different transfer question; thresholds are provisional teaching heuristics, not grades or diagnostic labels.
