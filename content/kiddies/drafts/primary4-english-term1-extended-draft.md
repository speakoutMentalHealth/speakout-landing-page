# Primary 4 English Studies — Term 1 extended original draft

**Editorial status:** Development draft only. Six original sample lessons; NOT a complete term or verified NERDC outcome map. Not approved by a qualified teacher or tested with learners.

**Access and safeguarding:** No child names, scores, audio recordings or identifiers are collected. All activities work on paper or orally. The named characters are fictional.

## Lesson 1 — Main idea and evidence

**Intended skill:** Main idea and evidence. Read, listen to or examine the original passage and explain an answer using its wording.

**Original reading / listening text:** Every Friday our class checks the school garden. Learners remove litter, water seedlings and record new leaves. The work helps us notice how plants change over time.

**Facilitator sequence:** Ask learners to justify answers using exact words from the passage. Invite individual thinking, paired discussion and non-competitive feedback. Model one response before independent practice.

**Interactive practice prompt:** Give the main idea and one supporting detail.

**Answer / evidence:** The class cares for and observes its garden; e.g. learners record new leaves.

**Printable worksheet:** (1) Give the main idea and one supporting detail. (2) Write a one-sentence summary and two supporting details. (3) Highlight or dictate a word or sentence from the passage that supports your response.

**Marking guidance:** Credit the stated answer and other evidence-based equivalents; give feedback on reasoning, not reading speed. For open-ended writing, check clarity, relevant details and appropriate punctuation rather than requiring identical wording.

**Accessibility and extension:** Offer large print, spoken response, reading partner, pointing, typing or handwriting. Learners may use a picture or sentence frame; confident learners explain a second interpretation supported by the text.
