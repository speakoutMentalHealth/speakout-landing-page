## Unit 9: Distinguishing evidence and inference

**Learning aim:** Read, discuss and apply distinguishing evidence and inference in a clear sentence or short response.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Recall a related skill from earlier units. Accept an oral, written or pointed response.

**Original classroom text/example:** The ground was wet, and three pupils carried umbrellas. The report does not mention rain.

**Teacher demonstration (10 minutes):** Read the example aloud, model the target skill and explain one choice in plain language.

**Guided question (10 minutes):** Can we conclude with certainty that it rained?

**Expected guided response:** No; wet ground may have another cause.

**Independent or supported practice (10 minutes):** Write one possible explanation and one additional fact you would need.

**Answer/review guidance:** A plausible explanation and evidence needed to confirm it. Accept accurate alternatives and give constructive private feedback.

**Reteach and exit check:** Model a shorter example, ask the pupil to try again, then invite one sentence explaining the key skill.

**Accessibility and safeguarding:** Offer home-language explanation, large print, read-aloud, dictation, drawing or pointing. Avoid personal disclosures, identifying data, public rankings and timed penalties.

## Unit 12: Critical reading of a notice

**Learning aim:** Read, discuss and apply critical reading of a notice in a clear sentence or short response.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Recall a related skill from earlier units. Accept an oral, written or pointed response.

**Original classroom text/example:** A poster says: 'All pupils prefer online lessons.' No survey results or source are given.
