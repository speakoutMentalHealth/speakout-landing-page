# Primary 3 Term 1 — mathematics foundation sequence (draft)

**Status:** Four provisional original teaching units, not a complete term or a verified NERDC/school-scheme alignment. Teacher approval and supervised pilot pending.

## Unit 1: Hundreds tens and ones

**Learning objective:** Learner demonstrates and explains the topic with age-appropriate speech, drawing, writing or pointing.

**Starter (5 min):** Discuss a familiar, safe example of hundreds tens and ones without collecting private experiences.

**Teacher modelling (10 min):** Use place-value columns and base-ten drawings.

**Guided practice (10 min):** In 356, what does the digit 5 represent? Invite explanation and provide extra thinking time.

**Independent worksheet (10 min):** Draw three hundred-squares, five ten-sticks and six dots; expand 356.

**Answer / marking guidance:** 5 tens, or 50. Accept equivalent correct answers and meaningful reasoning.

**Misconception check:** Reading 356 as 3 + 5 + 6.

**Exit assessment:** Ask the focus question again with a comparable example; record only anonymised teaching observations, not learner identifiers or public scores.

**Accessibility and safety:** Provide read-aloud instructions, large print, pointing, oral, drawing and seated alternatives. Do not time, rank or exclude pupils; adult-supervise practical activities and use no hazardous materials.

**Evidence gate:** Official NERDC outcome edition/page **unverified**; partner-school scheme **awaiting school**; qualified-teacher sign-off **not received**; supervised learner pilot **not conducted**.

## Unit 4: Simple fractions

**Learning objective:** Learner demonstrates and explains the topic with age-appropriate speech, drawing, writing or pointing.

**Starter (5 min):** Discuss a familiar, safe example of simple fractions without collecting private experiences.

**Teacher modelling (10 min):** Fold or draw equal sections first.

**Guided practice (10 min):** What fraction is one part of a shape divided into four equal parts? Invite explanation and provide extra thinking time.

**Independent worksheet (10 min):** Draw a rectangle, divide into four equal areas and shade one.

**Answer / marking guidance:** One quarter (1/4). Accept equivalent correct answers and meaningful reasoning.

**Misconception check:** Calling unequal parts quarters.
