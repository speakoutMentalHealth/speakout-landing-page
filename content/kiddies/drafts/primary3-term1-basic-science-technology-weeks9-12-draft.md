## Unit 9: Plant parts and functions

**Objective:** Explain the concept through observation, discussion and a simple example.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Recall a related lesson using a familiar object, picture or teacher drawing.

**Teacher model (10 minutes):** Roots take in water; leaves help plants make food using light. Explain the example clearly and invite learners to describe what they notice.

**Guided question (10 minutes):** Which part mostly absorbs water from soil?

**Answer guidance:** Roots.

**Independent or supported task (10 minutes):** Sketch a plant and label root, stem and leaf.

**Review guidance:** Correctly label three parts. Allow accurate equivalent responses.

**Exit ticket:** Give one real-world example of the concept and describe why it fits. Reteach with an easier illustration where necessary.

**Inclusion and safeguarding:** Permit pointing, dictation, drawings, seated participation and home-language scaffolding. Use adult-supervised, low-risk materials, and do not collect identifying learner information or rank pupils.

**Release gate:** Subject teacher must verify accuracy and appropriate Nigerian curriculum outcome, then record reviewer/date, accessibility and supervised trial evidence. All pending.
