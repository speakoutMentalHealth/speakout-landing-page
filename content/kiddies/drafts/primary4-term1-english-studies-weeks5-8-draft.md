## Week 5: Finding supporting detail

**Outcome:** Explain and apply the focus skill using an accessible response mode.

**Connect (5 min):** Revisit a relevant weeks 1–4 idea using a safe fictional example.

**Teacher modelling (10 min):** Work through a comparable example aloud with paper, visuals or safe local materials.

**Guided assessment (10 min):** Which sentence in a paragraph best proves its main idea? Ask for evidence or calculation steps.

**Printable worksheet (10 min):** Underline two supporting facts in a teacher-written passage.

**Answer / two-criterion rubric:** The one giving relevant evidence. Criterion A: accurate response; criterion B: clear supporting reasoning. Accept oral, written, pointing or drawn equivalents.

**Misconception / reteach:** Repeating the heading as evidence. Demonstrate a counterexample and offer a retry.

**Exit assessment (5 min):** Use an analogous scenario and give private feedback; record anonymised teaching patterns only.
