# Primary 2 — provisional weeks 1–4 tiered assessment drafts for five subjects

**Status:** Teacher-development prompts, not fully validated topic-specific examination items. Week allocations and outcome mapping require partner-school scheme and NERDC source verification. The questions below are age-adaptable, but qualified teachers must check level and curriculum fit.

**Rubric:** 1 point for a correct, safe or relevant answer; 1 point for an example, explanation, drawing or reason. 6 per topic. Accept accessible oral, written, drawn and pointing formats. Do not collect learner names or make high-stakes decisions.

## English studies

### Week 1: Read a sentence and identify its main detail

**Foundation question:** Read a sentence and identify its main detail. Give one correct example suitable for a Primary 2 pupil.

**Answer key:** Teacher checks for a relevant accurate example of read a sentence and identify its main detail; accept multiple culturally appropriate correct examples. Specific exemplar and distractors must be authored before learner release.

**Application question:** Look at a fictional classroom example of read a sentence and identify its main detail. Identify one appropriate action and explain why.

**Answer key:** Appropriate action plus a defensible reason; teacher must supply an actual fictional stimulus before use.

**Transfer question:** Imagine a different safe school or home situation involving read a sentence and identify its main detail. What would you do, and how would you explain your choice?

**Answer key:** Safe, fair and relevant decision justified in the new context; teacher must supply a concrete scenario before use.

**Marking:** 1 point for accurate response, 1 for relevant reasoning or representation. Open-response criteria require teacher moderation.

**Reteach:** Use a concrete picture or short fictional example of read a sentence and identify its main detail; model one reason and ask for a new example.

**Progress check:** Use a different example with no identifying pupil information; record anonymised teaching patterns.
