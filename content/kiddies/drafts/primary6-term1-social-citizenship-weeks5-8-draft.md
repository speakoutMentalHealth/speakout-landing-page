## Week 5: Responsible leadership

**Outcome:** Explain and apply the focus skill using an accessible response mode.

**Connect (5 min):** Revisit a relevant weeks 1–4 idea using a safe fictional example.

**Teacher modelling (10 min):** Work through a comparable example aloud with paper, visuals or safe local materials.

**Guided assessment (10 min):** How can a student leader report progress transparently? Ask for evidence or calculation steps.

**Printable worksheet (10 min):** Write a fictional class-project status report.

**Answer / two-criterion rubric:** Share accurate updates and invite questions. Criterion A: accurate response; criterion B: clear supporting reasoning. Accept oral, written, pointing or drawn equivalents.

**Misconception / reteach:** Leaders need not explain decisions. Demonstrate a counterexample and offer a retry.

**Accessibility and safeguarding:** Large print, read-aloud, visuals, oral/drawn responses and seated alternatives. No timed public rankings, learner personal data, unsupervised movement or hazardous materials.
