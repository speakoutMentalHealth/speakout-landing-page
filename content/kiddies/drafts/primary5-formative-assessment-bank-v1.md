# Primary 5 provisional formative assessment bank — six subjects

**Draft only:** Three sample questions per subject, not complete topic coverage, verified NERDC mapping or teacher-approved examination.

**Scoring guidance:** Each question: 1 point for a correct response and 1 point for explanation, working or relevant evidence. Maximum 6 per subject. Accept oral, drawn, written or pointing responses. Suggested private formative interpretation: 0–2 guided reteaching, 3–4 developing, 5–6 ready for a transfer item; these are provisional teaching cues, not learner labels.
