## Week 5: Community cooperation

**Learning outcome:** Explain and apply the idea through writing, speech, drawing or pointing.

**Connect (5 min):** Recall one earlier skill with a safe fictional example.

**Teacher modelling (10 min):** Demonstrate a worked example and narrate the steps; use local paper materials where possible.

**Guided assessment (10 min):** Why do neighbours work together on safe community projects? Invite reasoning and allow thinking time.

**Printable worksheet (10 min):** Plan a fictional adult-supervised awareness campaign.

**Marking guidance:** To solve shared problems. Two criteria: accurate result and justified reasoning. Accept equivalent oral, drawn and written responses.

**Misconception and reteaching:** Children should work unsupervised on roads. Show a counterexample and permit a retry.
