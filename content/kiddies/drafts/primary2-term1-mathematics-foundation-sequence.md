## Unit 1: Place value to 100

**Learning objective:** Learner demonstrates the focus skill by speaking, pointing, writing or drawing.

**Starter (5 min):** Ask a familiar, non-sensitive question about place value to 100 and show a safe picture or example.

**Teacher modelling (10 min):** Use bundles of ten and loose counters.

**Guided practice (10 min):** In 64, how many tens and ones are there? Invite learners to explain their thinking.

**Independent worksheet (10 min):** Draw six ten-sticks and four dots; write 64 in expanded form.

**Answer / marking guidance:** 6 tens and 4 ones. Accept equivalent accurate explanations and ask for reasoning.

**Misconception check:** Treating 64 as 6 + 4.

**Exit check:** Repeat the focus question in a fresh example; note anonymised learning gaps for lesson revision, not public ranking.

**Accessibility and safeguarding:** Offer large-print, oral, pointing, drawing and seated alternatives. No timer, names, stored learner scores, unsafe movement or required purchases. Adult supervision for practical activities.
