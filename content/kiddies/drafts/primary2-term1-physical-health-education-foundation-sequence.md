# Primary 2 Term 1 — physical health education provisional foundation sequence

**Status:** Four original sequenced foundation units; not a complete term or verified NERDC/school-scheme mapping. Teacher sign-off and learner pilot remain pending.

## Unit 1: Safe stretching

**Learning objective:** Learner demonstrates the focus skill by speaking, pointing, writing or drawing.

**Starter (5 min):** Ask a familiar, non-sensitive question about safe stretching and show a safe picture or example.

**Teacher modelling (10 min):** Offer seated and no-movement observation options.

**Guided practice (10 min):** Should a stretch be gentle or painful? Invite learners to explain their thinking.

**Independent worksheet (10 min):** Draw a comfortable stretch; write 'stop if it hurts'.

**Answer / marking guidance:** gentle. Accept equivalent accurate explanations and ask for reasoning.

**Misconception check:** Pushing through pain.

**Exit check:** Repeat the focus question in a fresh example; note anonymised learning gaps for lesson revision, not public ranking.

**Accessibility and safeguarding:** Offer large-print, oral, pointing, drawing and seated alternatives. No timer, names, stored learner scores, unsafe movement or required purchases. Adult supervision for practical activities.

## Unit 4: Rest and warning signs

**Learning objective:** Learner demonstrates the focus skill by speaking, pointing, writing or drawing.

**Starter (5 min):** Ask a familiar, non-sensitive question about rest and warning signs and show a safe picture or example.

**Teacher modelling (10 min):** Use discussion only, never induce symptoms.

**Guided practice (10 min):** What should you do if you feel dizzy during activity? Invite learners to explain their thinking.

**Independent worksheet (10 min):** Complete a safe-action sequence: stop, tell, rest.

**Answer / marking guidance:** Stop, rest and tell a trusted adult. Accept equivalent accurate explanations and ask for reasoning.

**Misconception check:** Ignoring dizziness to win.
