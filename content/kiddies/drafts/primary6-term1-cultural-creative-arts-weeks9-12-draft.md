# Primary 6 Term 1 cultural creative arts — provisional units 9–12

**Review-only draft:** Continues existing units 1–8; not a complete approved term or certified curriculum. Official subject designation, NERDC outcome/page, school scheme, qualified teacher sign-off and supervised pilot remain to be established.

## Unit 9: Visual hierarchy

**Learning objective:** Explain and demonstrate visual hierarchy through a simple classroom example.

**Teacher preparation:** Choose a familiar safe example, prepare a drawing or classroom object, and plan oral, visual and written response formats.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Invite learners to recall a related experience without requiring private information.

**Teacher modelling (10 minutes):** Explain the concept using a teacher-created example. Model respectful, safe participation.

**Guided question (10 minutes):** What should viewers notice first on an information poster?

**Expected response:** The main message or title.

**Independent or supported practice (10 minutes):** Learners create one additional example through a drawing, short sentence, demonstration or discussion, then explain why it fits the topic.

**Assessment and reteaching:** Check the explanation for understanding, not speed; model a simpler example and invite a retry if needed.

**Accessibility and safeguarding:** Use low-cost safe materials, avoid copying protected artwork, and credit cultural sources. Allow seated participation, home-language scaffolding and alternatives to speaking. Never rank pupils publicly or collect identifying data.

**Release gate:** Teacher must verify subject accuracy, class suitability, approved curriculum outcome and supervised pilot results. This unit remains draft and is not eligible for certificates.
