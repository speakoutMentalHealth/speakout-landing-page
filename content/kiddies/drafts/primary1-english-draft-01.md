## Accessible facilitation

Read every passage aloud on request, offer large print and accessible fonts, and allow speaking, signing, pointing, typing, writing or dictation. No public ranking or identifying learner data. Adapt reading level after teacher review; provide offline paper practice. Avoid treating a single response as proof of mastery.

## Lesson 1: Listening for sequence

**Goal:** Understand or communicate meaning from a short original text.

**Original passage or situation:** Ada packed a book. Next, she put on her shoes. Finally, she waved goodbye.

**Facilitator model:** Read slowly, ask learners to find clues, and model how to explain a choice with evidence from the passage.

**Interactive practice prompt:** Which happened immediately after Ada packed a book? Offer a spoken, signed, pointed or written response, then ask what in the passage supports it.

**Feedback and example answer:** She put on her shoes. Accept other valid wording and invite a retry if needed.

**Printable practice:** Draw three boxes and place the actions in order.

## Lesson 2: Naming and describing

**Goal:** Understand or communicate meaning from a short original text.

**Original passage or situation:** A small brown goat stood near a tree.

**Facilitator model:** Read slowly, ask learners to find clues, and model how to explain a choice with evidence from the passage.

**Interactive practice prompt:** Which two words describe the goat? Offer a spoken, signed, pointed or written response, then ask what in the passage supports it.

**Feedback and example answer:** Small and brown. Accept other valid wording and invite a retry if needed.

**Printable practice:** Draw the goat and label it with two describing words.

## Lesson 3: Asking clear questions

**Goal:** Understand or communicate meaning from a short original text.

**Original passage or situation:** Timi wants to know where the class library is.

**Facilitator model:** Read slowly, ask learners to find clues, and model how to explain a choice with evidence from the passage.

**Interactive practice prompt:** Write or say a polite question Timi could ask. Offer a spoken, signed, pointed or written response, then ask what in the passage supports it.

**Feedback and example answer:** Please, where is the class library? Accept other valid wording and invite a retry if needed.

**Printable practice:** Match a question mark to a question sentence.
