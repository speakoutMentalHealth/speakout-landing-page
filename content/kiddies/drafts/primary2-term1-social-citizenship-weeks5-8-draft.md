# Primary 2 term-one provisional weeks 5–8 — social citizenship

**Scope:** Four original extension units building on weeks 1–4 drafts. Not a complete term, official pacing, NERDC-verified alignment or school-approved curriculum.

## Week 5: Fair classroom rules

**Objective:** Learners answer the focus question and demonstrate reasoning using writing, speech, drawing or pointing.

**Connect (5 min):** Review a relevant earlier concept using a picture or paper example.

**Model (10 min):** Facilitator demonstrates the concept step by step with safe, locally available materials.

**Guided assessment (10 min):** Why should everyone have a turn to speak? Ask for reasoning and give specific private feedback.

**Printable worksheet (10 min):** Write two rules for respectful class discussion.

**Answer and rubric:** So everyone can participate fairly. Award one criterion for a correct response and one for a clear explanation; accept equivalent accessible modes. Do not publicly rank pupils.

**Misconception / reteach:** Only confident pupils deserve turns. Provide a second concrete example and invite a retry.

**Exit check (5 min):** Pose a similar question with altered details; use anonymised patterns to plan reteaching.

**Access and safety:** Read aloud, large print, visual support, oral/pointing/drawing and seated alternatives. No learner data collection, hazardous materials or unsupervised physical activity.

**Evidence gate:** NERDC outcome/page **unverified**; school scheme **awaiting school**; teacher approval **not received**; supervised pilot **not conducted**.
