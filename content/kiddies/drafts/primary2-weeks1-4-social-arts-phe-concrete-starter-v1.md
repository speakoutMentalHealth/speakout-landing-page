# Primary 2 social, arts and PHE — weeks 1–4 concrete assessment starter

**Provisional:** Three complete items per topic, with teacher review required. The four weekly slots are NOT verified against school schemes or NERDC.

## Social and citizenship

### Week 1: Foundations

**Foundation question:** What is a fair way to share one book?

**Expected answer:** Take turns.

**Application question:** In a fictional classroom, explain when this idea would help: What is a fair way to share one book?

**Expected answer:** Take turns. Include an appropriate classroom example.

**Transfer question:** In a different safe school setting, explain and demonstrate your answer to: What is a fair way to share one book?

**Expected answer:** Take turns. Include a relevant new-context explanation.

**Marking:** 1 for correct response and 1 for explanation or representation; allow oral, pointing and drawn responses.

**Reteach:** Model a concrete safe example and ask a fresh comparable question.

**Progress check:** Teacher observes anonymous group-level needs only.
