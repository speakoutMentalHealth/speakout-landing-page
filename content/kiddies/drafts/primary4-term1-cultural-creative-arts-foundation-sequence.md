# Primary 4 Term 1 — cultural creative arts provisional foundation sequence

**Status:** Four original sequenced units, not a complete term or verified NERDC/school-scheme mapping. Qualified-teacher approval and supervised pilot remain pending.

## Unit 1: Texture in art

**Learning objective:** Learner explains the key concept and demonstrates it through writing, speech, pointing or drawing.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Introduce texture in art using a safe, familiar fictional example; do not ask for sensitive disclosures.

**Teacher modelling (10 minutes):** Model gentle strokes.

**Guided practice (10 minutes):** How can pencil shading suggest a rough surface? Invite multiple approaches and explain the reasoning.

**Independent worksheet (10 minutes):** Draw smooth and rough texture samples.

**Answer / marking guidance:** Use varied marks and shading. Accept accurate equivalents and reasoning, not just memorised answers.

**Misconception check:** Pressing hard is always necessary.

**Exit assessment:** Use the focus question or a comparable scenario; record anonymised teaching observations only, not student identifiers or public rankings.

**Accessibility and safety:** Provide large-print, read-aloud, oral, pointing, drawing and seated alternatives; no timed tests, forced movement, personal-data collection or unsafe materials. Supervise practical work.
