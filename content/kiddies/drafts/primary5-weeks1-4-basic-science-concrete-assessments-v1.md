## Week 1: Food webs

**Foundation question:** What is a producer in a food web?

**Expected answer:** An organism such as a green plant that makes its own food.

**Marking:** 1 for correct science or safety response and 1 for appropriate reasoning; allow oral, drawn or written response.

**Application question:** In grass → grasshopper → frog, what eats the grasshopper?

**Expected answer:** The frog.

**Marking:** 1 for correct science or safety response and 1 for appropriate reasoning; allow oral, drawn or written response.

**Transfer question:** If many grasshoppers disappear, what may happen to frogs?

**Expected answer:** Frogs may have less food.

**Marking:** 1 for correct science or safety response and 1 for appropriate reasoning; allow oral, drawn or written response.

**Reteach:** Model a safe example, then check an unseen parallel scenario.
