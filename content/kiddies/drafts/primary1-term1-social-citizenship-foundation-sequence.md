# Primary 1 Term 1 — social citizenship foundation sequence

**Status:** Four provisional, original developmental units. Not a complete term, verified NERDC mapping or teacher-approved school scheme. School scheme integration deferred until documents arrive.

**Pacing:** One adaptable unit per week is a suggestion, not a school-approved timetable. Facilitate inclusive, noncompetitive learning.

## Unit 1: People who help us

**Observable objective:** Learners can demonstrate the main idea using speech, drawing, pointing or writing.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Invite a familiar example of people who help us; use a picture or teacher-provided object, never personal or sensitive disclosures.

**Teacher modelling (10 minutes):** Explain that pupils should approach designated, trusted school staff.

**Guided practice (10 minutes):** A pupil is lost in school. Which trusted adult could help? Allow thinking time and discuss more than one sensible response.

**Independent worksheet (10 minutes):** Draw a school helper; write or dictate what the helper does.

**Answer / success criterion:** A teacher or another designated school adult. Equivalent accurate, respectful responses are acceptable.

**Misconception to check:** Assuming every unfamiliar adult is safe.

**Exit question:** A pupil is lost in school. Which trusted adult could help? Invite the learner to explain the reason, without timed scoring or public ranking.

**Offline and accessible routes:** Large print, read-aloud instructions, paper drawing, oral responses, pointing, keyboard-friendly digital entry and adapted/seated movement as appropriate. Supervise movement and do not require paid materials.

## Unit 4: Kind words and listening

**Observable objective:** Learners can demonstrate the main idea using speech, drawing, pointing or writing.

**Starter (5 minutes):** Invite a familiar example of kind words and listening; use a picture or teacher-provided object, never personal or sensitive disclosures.

**Teacher modelling (10 minutes):** Role-play respectful turn-taking without sharing private experiences.

**Guided practice (10 minutes):** Your friend has a different idea for a game. What could you say? Allow thinking time and discuss more than one sensible response.

**Independent worksheet (10 minutes):** Complete: 'I can listen by ___'; draw two friends talking.

**Answer / success criterion:** I hear your idea; can we try taking turns?. Equivalent accurate, respectful responses are acceptable.

**Misconception to check:** Disagreement must become an argument.

**Exit question:** Your friend has a different idea for a game. What could you say? Invite the learner to explain the reason, without timed scoring or public ranking.
