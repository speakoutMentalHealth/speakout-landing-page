# Primary 6 Mathematics — original introductory draft

**Status:** Unapproved development preview; three sample lessons, not a complete scheme. Detailed NERDC outcome mapping, teacher review and learner pilot remain pending.

## Lesson 1

**Learning aim:** Explain and practise the skill in the question.

**Facilitator guide:** Introduce the scenario, model a worked example, ask the learner to explain their reasoning, and provide supportive feedback.

**Interactive practice prompt:** 35 of 100 library cards are new. What percentage?

**Answer key:** 35 percent.

**Printable worksheet:** Copy the question, draw or write the steps, and create one similar example with its answer.

**Worksheet answer guidance:** Compare the learner's result with the answer key and check the explanation, not just the final answer.

**Accessibility:** Allow spoken, typed, large-print, paper and manipulative responses. Do not require speed, personal information or student accounts.

## Facilitator upgrade — review-ready teaching protocol

**Preparation:** Use locally available number cards, counters, pencils and a number line. Check each worked example and adapt the numerical difficulty to the actual class before teaching.

**Lesson sequence:** Explain the objective; model an example with concrete objects; invite a guided attempt; allow quiet independent or supported practice; ask the lesson's existing check question; reteach with a smaller example where needed.

**Inclusive responses:** Accept spoken explanations, written working, pointing and drawings. Permit language scaffolding and additional thinking time; do not compare pupils publicly or require identifiable photographs.

**Quick exit check:** Ask each learner to show or explain one step from the lesson. Record only anonymous teaching observations, such as concepts requiring reteaching.

**Required release review:** A qualified Primary 6 mathematics teacher must verify every example and answer, map the lesson to the applicable Nigerian curriculum outcome, confirm accessibility, and approve a supervised trial. The current three lessons are introductory drafts, **not** a full term or school-year scheme. Keep publication and certification disabled pending review.
