# Primary 2 social citizenship — extended draft

**Status:** Six original developmental lesson outlines; not a complete term, not verified NERDC outcome mapping, not teacher-approved. These are inclusive, low-risk classroom activities; adapt to learner needs and school policies.

## Lesson 1 — Helping at home

**Learning aim:** Describe and apply helping at home in a familiar situation.

**Facilitator guidance:** Model the activity with words, images or safe objects; invite thoughtful, respectful responses. Avoid requiring learners to reveal personal or family information.

**Interactive question:** Name a helpful task a child can do safely

**Answer guidance:** Tidy books or put toys away. Accept equivalent sensible explanations.

**Printable worksheet:** (A) Name a helpful task a child can do safely (B) Draw or say one familiar example. (C) Write, draw or dictate a real-life example and discuss it with a facilitator.

**Assessment:** Check explanation and practical understanding, not speed or public ranking.

**Accessibility:** Large print, oral and written responses, keyboard-friendly digital prompts, no timed scoring, and appropriate physical adaptations.
