## Week 1: Living things

**Foundation question:** Which is a living thing: grass or a stone?

**Expected answer:** Grass.

**Marking:** 1 for scientifically accurate answer and 1 for relevant explanation or safe reasoning; accept oral, drawn or written responses.

**Application question:** Why do roots help a plant survive?

**Expected answer:** They absorb water and help anchor it.

**Marking:** 1 for scientifically accurate answer and 1 for relevant explanation or safe reasoning; accept oral, drawn or written responses.

**Transfer question:** A healthy plant wilts in dry soil. Suggest one safe change.

**Expected answer:** Water it appropriately and observe recovery.

**Marking:** 1 for scientifically accurate answer and 1 for relevant explanation or safe reasoning; accept oral, drawn or written responses.

**Reteach:** Demonstrate with a safe image or familiar observation, then ask a different question.
