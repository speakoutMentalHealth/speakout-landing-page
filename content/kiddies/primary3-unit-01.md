# Primary 3 Unit 1: Reading Clues and Number Strategies

## About this unit
Original SpeakOut introductory Primary 3 English Studies and Mathematics pack. Class placement is a proposed teaching level, not verified alignment to individual Nigerian NERDC class outcomes. No teacher approval or supervised trial is claimed. Earlier classes remain available for revision, and the material does not constitute a complete term.

## Facilitator preparation and inclusion
Prepare safe paper drawings and a notebook. Read text aloud if needed, explain unfamiliar vocabulary in a learner's familiar language, and accept speech, signing, pointing, dictation or other communication aids. Provide breaks, adapt the quantities and do not require personal disclosures. Check understanding with a new example after modelling. Suggested lesson length is 15–25 minutes, adapted to needs.

## English Lesson 1: Find story evidence

Aim: practise find story evidence using an original worked example.
Model: Amina puts the washed cup on a tray before sweeping the floor. Explain the key concept carefully and demonstrate it on paper.
Together: ask the learner to explain or point to the clue in the example. Revisit prerequisite skills where needed and model the answer once without pressure.
Try: What did Amina do first? Ask the learner to show how they know, not only tap a correct choice. Then invite a fresh example using new names, numbers or drawings.
Check and answer: She put the cup on a tray. Use the order of events stated in the sentence. Record whether help was needed.
Next step: repeat with a different short example, checking the reason behind the learner's response.
