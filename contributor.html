How Approval Works

Quality first.

  1. You submit your contributor application.
  2. SpeakOut reviews your profile, sample content and topic fit.
  3. If approved, you become a verified contributor.
  4. Admin can publish your approved books or courses.
  5. Premium revenue sharing can be connected later when payment is enabled.
Publishing and payment access remain admin-controlled.
  • Original educational content only
  • Reviewed before publication
  • Audience and safety fit required
  • Contributor status must be approved
