# National online baseline

The public `national-curriculum.html` guide uses official online NERDC subject offerings and the FME reform announcement. Data is in `js/national-curriculum.js`, checked 10 October 2026. Shared subjects are listed once, with class-group differences. This is a subject framework, not a delivered lesson catalogue or verification of any material.

The NERDC offerings PDF has no printed publication date, so it is not assigned a fabricated edition date. Its subject structure is consistent with the revised framework announced for 2025/26. No exact subject-count requirement is enforced: selection rules and definitive implementation details belong to the official authorities, and the reform announcement contains differing upper-primary count statements. The guide includes JSS Business Studies from the NERDC offering.

Primary/JSS/SS classes map to the existing open education stages. No access restrictions, production migrations, content publication or approval changes are introduced. Nursery follows the separately inspected 2024-reviewed pre-primary guide; tertiary remains qualification/programme specific.

Detailed revised outcomes remain pending legitimate source access. Do not treat old official documents, third-party schemes or a subject title as complete current outcome evidence. Schools may review original activities; their customised syllabus is not required. SpeakOut term sequences must be labelled proposed delivery plans. Rights and human educational review remain pending for the draft starter materials. No official PDF is redistributed.
