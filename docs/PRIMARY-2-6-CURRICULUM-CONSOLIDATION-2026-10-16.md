## Known issues: must resolve before release
1. **Assessment misalignment:** Existing `content/kiddies/drafts/assessment-quality-progression-audit-20261011.md` records cases where the proposed lesson objective and the assessment-bank objective differ (example: Primary 4 maths week 6). Do not silently relabel either record.
2. **Draft activity versus assessment:** The units 9–12 contain formative checks, but **do not** yet have validated, standalone teacher-marked item banks and full-credit/partial-credit examples. They cannot serve as summative assessments.
3. **Progression:** Compare Primary 2 concrete representations with Primary 3 reasoning, Primary 4 multi-step explanations, and Primary 5–6 justification. Avoid superficial vocabulary increases without genuine conceptual development.
4. **Subjects:** Confirm the applicable NERDC/school subject labels, especially Basic Science at Primary 1–3 versus Basic Science and Technology at Primary 4–6. Do not use a national subject list as evidence of detailed outcome alignment.
5. **Rights and safeguards:** Confirm originality and permissions for all stimuli, culturally appropriate examples and adult-supervised activity adaptations. Do not record children's names, individual scores, diagnoses or identifiable images in review artifacts.
6. **Term completion:** Units labelled 1–12 are not proof of a complete term, all required subjects, instructional hours or official pacing.

## Teacher review sequence
1. School supplies its applicable approved scheme and curriculum edition.
2. Subject teacher chooses one class and strand and maps each unit to a specific official outcome.
3. Teacher reviews every example and answer, then marks corrections in a version-controlled review log.
4. Match each formative question to its taught objective; author missing evidence-based marking exemplars.
5. Conduct supervised, accessible testing with non-identifiable class-level observations.
6. Editorial lead signs off only the validated unit versions; perform deployment tests before import.
