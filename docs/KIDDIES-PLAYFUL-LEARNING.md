# Kiddies playful learning — 10 October 2026

The original Nursery and Primary 1 units now have an interactive learner layer: ten named lesson adventures, twenty original two-choice/three-choice practice questions, visible counting circles, shape pictures, comparison groups, joining groups and crossed-out subtraction circles. Children can answer with a mouse, touch or keyboard, or direct an adult to select an answer. A correct selection reveals an explanation and the next activity; another selection gives a helpful explanation and allows unlimited retries. There is no timer, leaderboard, penalty, paid reward or certification.

Each lesson retains its full original aim, modelling, supported practice, independent attempt, answers and next teaching step in the grown-up guide. Worksheets and observation/preparation guidance remain available through the section selector. Printing includes the full selected lesson's teaching notes and hides interactive controls. The same SpeakOut navy, blue and gold identity is used for the playful hub, catalogue cards and reader.

The journey map leaves every lesson available. Exploring both questions earns one exploration star for that lesson; replaying does not add duplicate stars. A final Curious Explorer message acknowledges exploration, not independent mastery, developmental achievement or national curriculum completion. Local progress is optional and untrusted. It stores only completed lesson section IDs under an account/unit/version namespace in browser storage; no individual answers, attempts, school grades, observation notes, mental-health disclosures or analytics are stored or sent. Different signed-in accounts use different keys. Blocked/corrupt storage falls back to progress for the current visit. Clearing stars requires an explicit second click, scoped to the current unit.

The published catalogue/readiness and canonical role/approval guard remain intact. Nursery/Primary labels are demarcations; browsing another level does not change school placement or saved preferences. Content-source/mapping limits, AI-assisted authorship and pending independent teacher validation remain visible. This interaction layer does not improve the evidence level of the underlying curriculum mapping.

The Kiddies hub features the two real available units, with its existing resource catalogue preserved. It does not promise that Primary 2–6 programmes or every existing supplementary resource have been converted into the new adventures. Subsequent work can extend this reusable format after actual new material and classroom feedback are available.

Validation covers twenty question/answer definitions, picture quantities and arithmetic answers, all six Primary adventures end to end, Nursery retries and completion, reload/reset progress, blocked storage, corrupt/foreign-account storage, canonical pending-account redirect, mobile/desktop accessibility, print visibility and existing catalogue/browser regressions. Browser account and database interactions use fixtures; no real child account or observation data is used.
