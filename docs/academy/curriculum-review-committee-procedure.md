# SpeakOut Academy — Curriculum Review Committee (proposed procedure)

**Status:** Proposed; no school nominations, teacher approvals, curriculum editions, or learner pilot are evidenced by this document. This process does not publish content or change access controls.

## Membership and independence
Invite each of SpeakOut's three partner schools to nominate one or two qualified teachers, with subject, class-level experience and school affiliation documented. Name a school academic coordinator and a SpeakOut documentation coordinator. Health-college specialists may review technical health accuracy, but do not replace primary-school pedagogy review. Disclose conflicts; the author of a lesson must not be its sole approving reviewer. Participation is voluntary and requires school confirmation.

## Pilot protocol (only after school permission)
Record school authorization, consent/assent requirements, safeguarding lead, facilitator, accessibility accommodations and the exact lesson version. Avoid identifying learners in GitHub, videos or public issues. Collect only anonymized observations: comprehension, accessibility, engagement and corrections needed. Do not require disclosure of personal health experiences. Record pilot date, class level, approximate attendance, reviewer and next decision in school-controlled records. Public tracking should include only non-identifying status.

## Release gates
1. Independent dated version-specific teacher decision recorded.
2. Curriculum edition and detailed outcome references verified or explicitly declared unmapped.
3. School-authorized supervised pilot and documented corrections, when required by release policy.
4. Automated tests, security checks, accessibility and browser checks passing.
5. Separate approved PR for learner catalogue publication; preserve existing student content and access controls.

## School invitation
Subject: Request for Curriculum Review Partnership — SpeakOut Academy

Dear Headteacher/Principal,

As part of our ongoing educational partnership, SpeakOut is developing the **SpeakOut Academy**, a digital learning platform designed to provide engaging, accessible and interactive educational materials for learners.

We would like to invite your school to participate in reviewing and strengthening the Primary 1–6 learning materials currently under development.

We kindly request your support in two areas:

1. **Curriculum verification:** Share or confirm the current curriculum, scheme of work and subject requirements used by your school.
2. **Teacher participation:** Nominate one or two qualified teachers to review selected learning materials for accuracy, age appropriateness and curriculum alignment.

This collaboration will help ensure that SpeakOut Academy complements classroom teaching and responds to the actual learning needs of students.

We intend to begin with a small, structured review and expand gradually, working closely with our partner schools.

We look forward to your participation in building a valuable educational resource for our communities.

**SpeakOut Team**
