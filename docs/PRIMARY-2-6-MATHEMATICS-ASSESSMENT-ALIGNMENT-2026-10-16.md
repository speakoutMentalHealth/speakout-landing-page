## Priority editorial blockers

1. **Primary 5 week 6:** Lesson is explicitly **Fraction multiplication**, but the draft assessment uses 1/3 of 18, 2/3 of 18, and 2/3 of 24 pupils. These are fraction-of-whole-number tasks and may not establish the intended multiplication objective. Keep existing source unchanged until the school scheme determines whether fraction multiplication means a fraction of a whole number or a fraction multiplied by a fraction; then align objectives and assessment transparently.
2. **Primary 3 week 8:** A fractions-on-a-number-line lesson may not be represented by generic fraction questions. Inspect each item and add a number-line stimulus where missing.
3. **Primary 6 week 5:** A ratio-tables lesson needs tabular representation; evaluate question formats.
4. **Primary 4 week 6:** Updated alignment exists and now needs independent mathematics-teacher review.

## Marking-quality gate

Every item must include a reproducible numerical or visual stimulus, correct answer, observable reasoning criterion, full-credit/partial-credit/error examples, and age-appropriate accessible alternatives. Correctness must be reviewed independently; superficial heading agreement alone does not qualify an item for release.
