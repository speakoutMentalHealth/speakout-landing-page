# Nursery and Primary 1 Unit 3 — 10 October 2026

The next published batch adds Nursery observation activities and Primary 1 Basic Science to the existing language/number units. The collection now has six introductory units, thirty lessons and sixty original interactive questions. Unit numbering remains an optional SpeakOut sequence, with open browsing across education levels.

Every lesson has an aim, model, supported practice, attempt, answers and next step. Adult preparation, accessible response routes and private observation guidance are retained in the learner edition. Nursery includes a reusable activity sheet; Primary includes six separately formatted science tasks and an answer/support section. Paper activities require no purchase, child account or outdoor trip. Fictional observations are labelled; no real learner experiment, growth schedule or teacher approval is invented.

Interactive practice includes an original CSS plant drawing with a screen-reader description and two fair-comparison strip diagrams. Both strips start at the same line; the second question uses equal lengths and different widths. There are no timers, penalties or required touch. Stars remain browser-local participation markers isolated by account/unit/version. Earlier units, source sections and progress namespaces are preserved.

## Editorial/source review and limits

Review checked object versus material examples, observation/unknown distinctions, living classifications, the limits of movement as evidence of life, plant simplifications, fair comparison, task/answer consistency and accessible paper alternatives. Plant activities use adult drawings without tasting, picking, uprooting or handling unknown objects. Digital questions recognise supported choices; independent science explanation remains for actual adult observation.
