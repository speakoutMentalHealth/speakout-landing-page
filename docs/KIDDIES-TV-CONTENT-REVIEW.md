# Kiddies TV content review

Audience: Nigerian and wider African families; ages 3–5, 6–9, 10–12 and 13–17. Africa has varied cultures; use a clear educational standard rather than claiming one universal cultural rule.

For every video, a named adult reviewer must watch the full video and record the exact URL, publisher, duration, review date, age band, learning objective, and approval decision before publishing. Record captions and Nigerian playback checks, including the ending. Metadata or channel reputation alone is insufficient.

Exclude sexual content, nudity, romantic/kissing scenes regardless of orientation, graphic violence, dangerous imitation, profanity, hate, discriminatory messages, substance promotion, scams and misleading claims. Do not label people or identities as harmful. Prioritise curriculum skills, practical development, respectful family/community values and African relevance. Avoid stereotypes.

YouTube ads, links and recommendations remain outside SpeakOut’s full control. Keep caregiver guidance visible. Do not promise an ad-free or completely risk-free service.

## Teen candidates
`data/kiddies-tv-teen-candidates.json` is a non-publication queue. These videos were discovered through publisher/YouTube search results, not fully watched or approved. Do not copy them into the approved catalogue until full review and device playback verification are recorded. No new teen videos are live yet.

## Reports
Library tiles and approved watch pages prepare a report to the contact channels already listed on SpeakOut’s contact page. They include video URL, title and selected concern only. The adult must review and send; opening WhatsApp/email is not receipt or resolution. No report is stored by this UI. Existing SpeakOut contact administrators receive messages; a dedicated curator, response expectations and coverage still need assignment. For a credible safeguarding concern, remove the catalogue entry or set approved=false while investigating, publish through the normal release process, and verify removal on the live site. Preserve private details outside GitHub.

## Weekly publishing
Assign a primary curator and backup, review candidates before weekly publication, and recheck existing videos and reports. Weekly additions are an operational commitment, not an automatic capability.
