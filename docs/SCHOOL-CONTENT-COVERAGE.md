# School content coverage and release readiness

Updated 10 October 2026. This is a coverage register, not a claim of a complete Nigerian national programme. Unit numbering is a SpeakOut suggestion; labels help browsing and do not restrict access between levels. Existing tertiary/enrichment materials remain available under their existing classifications.

Counts above refer to these six introductory units only. A course outline, subject heading or gamified recognition activity does not complete a subject. Digital correct choices do not prove independent explanation, reading, mathematical reasoning or mastery. Paper practice and actual supported responses guide teaching.

## Next authoring batches

1. Broaden Nursery creative expression, supported communication and shared routines. Extend Primary 1 English and Mathematics from observed learner needs and the current detailed outcomes when available.
2. Build Primary 1 subject breadth with qualified subject review, preserving optional subjects and appropriate faith/language choices. Do not create false universal religious or language requirements.
3. Develop Primary 2 and 3 with distinct class placement, then Primary 4–6 using their framework. Each class needs its own coverage register; a changed class label is not a new lesson.
4. Audit and develop secondary class/pathway materials; review existing tertiary material by programme and regulator.

These are planned batches, not completed work or a guaranteed national timetable. Existing materials must be reviewed before reclassification or publication; do not overwrite administrator records to fill coverage gaps.

## Evidence required before describing a complete programme as ready

For each unit record the actual source edition, subject/class outcome, page or outcome identifier, mapping rationale and any remaining gap. A broad thematic or subject-heading relationship remains partial/unverified. Current detailed Primary outcomes are not established by the subject-offerings PDF.

Complete editorial review of aims, examples, language, answers, activity instructions, local context and factual accuracy. Obtain an independent teacher's dated decision for the unit version. Record requested corrections and close blocking content/safety/accessibility issues. Run a supervised trial with suitable learners and document anonymised issues, response routes and support; do not invent participants or feedback.

Check catalogue placement, open cross-level browsing, approval enforcement, print/offline usability, keyboard and screen-reader behaviour, small-screen layout, retry/progress behaviour and privacy. Automated fixture tests establish software behaviour, not classroom effectiveness. Keep original teaching notes and answer guidance available to adults, and mark exploration rewards as participation.

Use draft / editorially checked / teacher reviewed / trial checked / published as separate evidence fields in the review record. The present introductory units are published with AI-assisted editorial checks, while independent teacher validation and a supervised trial remain pending. Publication alone does not change those fields.
