# Nursery and Primary 1 catalogue release — 10 October 2026

Published two original introductory units through the versioned website catalogue. Nursery appears across nursery classes with adaptable placement; Primary appears for Primary 1 and All primary classes. Both appear in Courses and units and in Materials (the same unit's practice sheets, not separate duplicated courses). Changing section/class immediately updates the catalogue without saving a preference or changing a school record. All learners can browse other levels.

## Teaching and editorial review

AI-assisted review checked each lesson's aim, model, supported practice, independent attempt, answer guidance and next step against the original content. All explicit arithmetic equations and worksheet answers were checked. Story questions distinguish stated facts from unknown details. Phonics examples distinguish letter names from sounds, avoid extra consonant vowels and use the sat/tap sequence only. Local pronunciation and teacher placement validation remain pending. Materials use familiar large safe objects/drawings, short suggested sessions, home-language explanation, alternative communication and pauses. They collect no learner observations or personal disclosures. Observation guidance is private, non-diagnostic and ungraded.

The learner edition removes draft workflow paragraphs while retaining the lessons, worksheets, answers, preparation and observation guidance. It displays the editorial review limits and does not claim human approval, official endorsement, accreditation, completion certification or full-programme coverage. Original AI-assisted text was checked for copied national passages; no curriculum PDF is redistributed. This is not a legal originality clearance or an independent classroom trial.

## Publication and maintenance

These are explicitly short introductory units with their own published schema, not full course/book records. Existing full-course length/readiness gates, Firestore publication rules and administrator edits remain unchanged. Publication is through reviewed git changes, not a production Firestore seed/import. Remove a unit from publication by changing its status in the catalogue generator and rebuilding/redeploying. Units remain available when remote course/book reads fail, alongside a visible partial-load warning. Reader navigation uses the existing canonical role/approval guard; published text is public static educational content, not confidential or paid content.
