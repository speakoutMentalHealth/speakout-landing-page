# Nigerian curriculum implementation register

Source register: `js/curriculum-mapping.js`, checked 10 October 2026. The register distinguishes an inspected edition from an authority/portal identified for subsequent research. It is not evidence of complete curriculum coverage.

School sections use NERDC for pre-primary, Primary, JSS and SS. University programmes require NUC programme standards and institution schemes; technical qualifications require the applicable NBTE programme curriculum; NCE requires NCCE standards. Professional qualifications also require their applicable regulator. Tertiary class labels alone do not establish programme alignment.

The draft Nursery book has four candidate partial references to the 2024-reviewed pre-primary document. PDF pages are one-based, with printed page numbers recorded separately. Counting is deliberately unmapped. The book does not cover all developmental domains or a full-year programme. The national one-year pre-primary scope must not be equated automatically with Nursery 1–3. Human class placement, accessibility, observation checks and term sequencing remain pending.

Primary has an official portal reference only. Full class/subject outcomes and edition have not been verified, so no chapter alignment is claimed. Senior and tertiary materials are not authored by this change.

Both starter books and both outlines remain draft, pending teacher review and `curriculumAlignment: not-verified`. The import validator rejects verified/full-coverage claims within this draft format. Existing imported IDs are never overwritten. This is not a new universal server-side publishing approval gate; ordinary admin publishing controls still require separate release work if mandatory editorial approval is desired.

Next: obtain applicable class/subject documents, record outcome locators and gaps, map each chapter/lesson, agree school term schemes, author missing content, collect real teacher/rights review, then release assessed material. Do not fabricate approval records. School curriculum content must remain distinguishable from supplementary wellbeing, digital and leadership content; no legacy item is retroactively labelled aligned. A full catalogue/editor classification workflow remains follow-up work.

No official curriculum PDF is copied into the repository. Its copyright notice requires permission for redistribution. Original activities remain independently authored; references point to the official source. Curriculum metadata does not affect access: Nursery/Primary remain within Kiddies Corner, and learners can browse other educational levels.
