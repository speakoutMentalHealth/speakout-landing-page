# Nursery and Primary 1 Unit 5

Original introductory units, authored and editorially checked with AI assistance on 10 October 2026. Independent teacher validation and a supervised learner trial remain pending. These materials extend a suggested SpeakOut sequence, not a national timetable or prerequisite.

## Published content

- Nursery: **Talk, Listen and Tell**, 1,804 source words, four lessons and eight practice questions. Requests for help/pause, on/under drawings, supported two-step directions and a fictional three-event retelling. Complete adult modelling, practice, checks, next steps and a reusable paper sheet with answers.
- Primary 1: **Read, Ask and Solve**, 2,708 source words, six lessons and twelve practice questions. Story evidence, questions and end marks, a small pin/tin/pit/sit blending set, number order from zero to ten, two parts of ten and represented problems within ten. Separate English and Mathematics sheets with three tasks each and full answer/support guidance.
- Overall collection: ten introductory units, fifty lessons and one hundred original interactive questions. Primary 1 English and Mathematics now have nine lessons each across Units 1, 2 and 5. Other subjects and classes remain incomplete; see SCHOOL-CONTENT-COVERAGE.md.

## Teaching and source checks

Check fictional story details, pronoun references and order; do not invent a book colour. Distinguish statements/questions by meaning, with appropriate written end marks. Blend short i with adult modelling, distinguish letter names from sounds, avoid added vowels in stop consonants, and explain meanings through safe pictures. The shared story contains words beyond the small phonics set and is for adult-supported reading. Button recognition does not prove independent reading.

Check all number examples through the represented action: 6 + 4 = 10; 7 + 3 = 10; 4 + 6 = 10; 6 + 2 = 8; 9 − 3 = 6; 5 + 3 = 8; 8 − 2 = 6. Immediately before/after identifies the neighbouring label. Pictured circle counts and crossed-out quantities must match the answers.

Nursery has partial relationships to the 2024-reviewed NERDC pre-primary language domain, printed pp.27–30 / PDF pp.44–47, for responding, supported directions and retelling: https://ppec.nerdcportals.com.ng/pre_primary/web/ppec_tg.pdf . Exact lesson alignment is not established; position/help examples are original SpeakOut aims. Primary English Studies/Mathematics match subject headings only: https://nerdc.gov.ng/content_manager/pdf_files/Basic%20and%20Senior%20Secondary%20Education%20Curriculum%20Offerings.pdf . The revised Primary 1 link from the official curriculum entry returned an unreadable error during this review; detailed outcomes were not verified. Older framework pages and commercial schemes are not substituted for current detailed outcome evidence.

Requests, directions and retelling accept established communication aids, signs, dictation or adult-directed actions. Help and breaks are not conditional on a polite formula or English speech. Directions are invitations to a safe activity; no general obedience claim. No forced movement, crawling under furniture, real sharp pins or pit visits. Home-language explanation is welcome. Planning notes stay private; no recordings, addresses or disclosures are requested.
