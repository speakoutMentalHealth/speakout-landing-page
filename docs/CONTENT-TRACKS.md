# School curriculum and supplementary resources

`contentTrack` is an explicit editorial classification: `school-curriculum`, `supplementary`, or blank (not yet classified). Legacy records are not classified from their subject, education stage or category. When no top-level field exists, an existing `curriculum.track` supplies the draft pack's classification; an explicit blank overrides it.

The course and book editors load/save this field. Merge writes retain existing outcome-mapping evidence; clearing or switching the classification does not erase that evidence. Classification is not an accreditation or approval workflow. School cards always state that alignment is not established: unverified admin strings must not issue a verified badge. Real outcome verification and a mandatory server-side editorial approval gate remain separate follow-up work.

My Learning defaults to all content types. Optional school, supplementary and unclassified filters apply equally to courses/materials and update counts. They do not change default placement, approval, school records or eligibility. Cross-level browsing stays open. Draft/unready content is still excluded by existing visibility checks. No production records are migrated or republished.

An authorised editor should classify existing records individually against their actual purpose and evidence. Keep wellbeing, leadership and digital resources as enrichment where appropriate; do not bulk infer classification from category. The Primary official outcome-document access limitation remains unresolved.
