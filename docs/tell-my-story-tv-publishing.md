# Tell My Story — SpeakOut TV publishing

## Page structure
- TV home spotlight: \`tv.html\` → Tell My Story → \`pages/tell-my-story.html\`
- Partner profile, official links and SHIFT information: \`pages/tell-my-story.html\`
- Approved videos from the existing media API / Firestore TV collection: \`js/tell-my-story-page.js\`
- Shared visual styling: \`css/tv/tell-my-story.css\`

## Publish future interviews and trailers
1. Open the existing authenticated SpeakOut TV Studio (\`admin-tv.html\`).
2. Upload a **licensed/authorised** video link and title, description, and artwork. Keep drafts unpublished during review.
3. Add **tell-my-story** to the Tags field (alongside other relevant tags, e.g. \`tell-my-story, student-stories, youth\`). You may also use \`Tell My Story\` as the series/show name.
4. Confirm interview consent, safeguarding checks, editing approval, and any third-party copyright permissions before setting Visibility to **Published**.
5. Check \`pages/tell-my-story.html\`. The public collection automatically lists matching published/active TV episodes and links them back to the existing SpeakOut TV player.

Content marked hidden/draft is excluded. No student interview is recorded or posted solely for promotion if the student seeks private mental health support. For minors, secure appropriate guardian consent and safeguarding review.

## SHIFT screening
The dedicated page **links** to Tell My Story's official SHIFT page but does not upload, embed or re-stream the full film. Arrange screening rights and obtain the approved film asset directly from Tell My Story.
