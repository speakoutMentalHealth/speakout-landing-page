## Updating the catalogue

A content editor must update the relevant `data/home-learning-catalog.json` entry **only after** the signed record has been reviewed and safely retained in the internal editorial system. Do not publish a reviewer's personal signature or contact details. Approved status requires `reviewer`, `reviewed_at` and `curriculum_reference`. For combined JSS 1–3 or SS 1–3 catalogue entries, **all three underlying class packs** must pass before the group may be shown as approved. Otherwise leave it unreviewed or in review.

This record is for trained educators and reviewers, not a self-certification checklist for publishing.
