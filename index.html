About SpeakOut

We are building a safer ecosystem for young people to speak, learn and thrive.

SpeakOut began with one belief: young people should not have to reach crisis before someone notices they are struggling. Our work connects mental health awareness, early support, education, media, school structures and digital access.

  • Mental health education that is simple and practical
  • School clubs that keep the conversation alive
  • Digital tools and resources for role-based support
  • Future skills through SpeakHub Academy
