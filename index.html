Why We Exist

Mental Health Is Still Silent For Many Young Africans

Many students and young people carry stress, anxiety, depression, trauma, loneliness, family pressure, academic pressure, and financial pressure without a safe place to speak or receive support.

We break the silence.

SpeakOut creates safe conversations in schools, communities, faith spaces, workplaces, and digital platforms so people can understand what they feel and know where to start.

We connect people to tools.

Through MindCheck, users can privately assess their mental wellness, access coping resources, learn skills, and connect with support channels.

We build young leaders.

Our volunteer and ambassador network gives young people a platform to lead, serve, advocate, and carry mental health awareness into their communities.

Program Zones

How SpeakOut Helps

We combine mental health education, digital support, outreach, training, and community engagement into one clear ecosystem.

🏫

School Outreach

Class-to-class and campus-based mental health awareness for students, colleges, universities, and youth communities.

🧠

Private Mental Checks

Users can check their mood, stress, anxiety, depression indicators, and emotional wellness through MindCheck.

🏢

Workplace Wellness

Training for teams on stress, burnout, emotional safety, productivity, and mental wellness in the workplace.

🏥

Healthcare Staff Training

Mental health education, staff wellness training, and certificate-based programs for healthcare workers and institutions.

⛪

Faith & Community Sessions

Churches, mosques, community groups, and local leaders can host SpeakOut sessions for their members.

📚

Digital Learning

E-library, wellness tips, coping skills, ICT training, healthcare learning, and professional development resources.
