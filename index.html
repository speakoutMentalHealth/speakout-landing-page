Who We Are

SpeakOut is building a safer mental health culture for young people.

We work with schools, communities, parents, teachers and youth leaders to reduce stigma, promote early support, and create safe spaces where young people can learn, speak and receive guidance.

  • Mental health awareness and education
  • School mental health clubs
  • Parent and teacher workshops
  • Digital learning through SpeakHub, E-Library and Kiddies Corner
  • Certificates for trainings and programs

Our belief

Mental health support should not begin only when a young person is already in crisis. It should begin with awareness, safe conversations, trusted adults, practical tools and early guidance.

SpeakOut turns mental health education into a structured school and community ecosystem.

Why It Matters

Mental health silence is costing young people too much.

Many students struggle with stress, fear, bullying, low confidence, anxiety, family pressure, academic pressure and emotional pain without knowing where to turn.

😔

Students suffer quietly

Many young people do not speak because of shame, fear, punishment or lack of trusted support.

🏫

Schools need structure

Schools may care deeply but still lack a simple, organized model for mental health education and club activities.

👨‍👩‍👧

Adults need tools

Parents and teachers need practical guidance to identify warning signs, support children and respond without stigma.

How It Works

🏫

Schools

For Schools

A complete school ecosystem, not just a one-day talk.

SpeakOut helps schools create a sustainable mental health structure that includes students, teachers, parents and school leadership.

  • School applies and gets approved by SpeakOut
  • School receives a school code and dashboard
  • School adds teachers, parents and students
  • School approves its own members
  • School tracks learning, workshops and certificates
