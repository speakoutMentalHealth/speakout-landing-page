How It Works

The SpeakOut school journey

Schools are approved by SpeakOut. Then each school controls only its own teachers, parents and students.

School Applies

The school submits its application and waits for SpeakOut review.

SpeakOut Approves

Super Admin approves the school and generates a school code.

School Onboards Users

The school adds or approves teachers, parents and students.

Learning Begins

Users access role-specific resources, courses and certificates.
