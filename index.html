Mental Health Tip of the Week

Don’t wait for a breakdown before asking “Are you okay?”

A simple check-in can interrupt isolation. Ask gently, listen without rushing, and help the person identify one safe next step.

For Students

When pressure feels heavy, talk to one trusted person today. Silence makes stress feel stronger.

For Parents

Children may hide distress behind anger, withdrawal or poor performance. Lead with listening.

For Teachers

A change in behavior is communication. Notice, document, support and refer when necessary.
