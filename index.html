Why We Exist

Mental Health Is Still Silent For Many Young Africans

Many students and young people carry stress, anxiety, depression, trauma, loneliness, family pressure, academic pressure and financial pressure without a safe place to speak or receive support.

We break the silence.

We create safe conversations in schools, communities, workplaces and digital platforms.

We connect people to tools.

Through MindCheck, users can privately check in, learn coping skills and access support resources.

We build young leaders.

Our volunteer and ambassador network helps young people lead, serve and advocate.
