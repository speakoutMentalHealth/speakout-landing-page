Featured Campaign

School mental health clubs that outlive one-day awareness talks.

Awareness matters, but schools need more than one event. SpeakOut helps schools build mental health clubs where students, teachers and parents can keep learning, discussing, reporting concerns, joining workshops and participating in wellbeing activities.

The goal is simple: make emotional wellbeing a normal part of school life, not a hidden topic students only talk about when things become serious.

  • Student-led awareness activities
  • Teacher coordinator support
  • Parent engagement and education
  • Workshops, certificates and school reports

Mental Health Education

What every young person should know.

Mental health is not only about crisis. It is about how young people think, feel, cope, relate with others, respond to pressure and ask for support when life becomes heavy.

🧠

Stress is a signal

Stress can show up as headaches, tiredness, anger, fear, silence, poor sleep or loss of interest. It is the body and mind asking for attention.

Learn Stress Signs
💬

Speaking early helps

Many young people wait until they are overwhelmed before talking. Speaking early can prevent isolation and help someone find safer next steps.

Find Help Pathways
🤝

Support is strength

Asking for help is not weakness. It is a responsible step toward healing, clarity, stability and better decision-making.

Mental Health Tip of the Week

Don’t wait for a breakdown before asking “Are you okay?”

A simple check-in can interrupt isolation. Ask gently, listen without rushing, and help the person identify one safe next step.

For Students

When pressure feels heavy, talk to one trusted person today. Silence makes stress feel stronger, but support can help you breathe again.

For Parents
