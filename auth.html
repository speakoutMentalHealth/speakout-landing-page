Register a School

Bring SpeakOut to your institution

Register your institution for SpeakHub learning, student development, wellbeing programmes and the SpeakOut School Portal. The primary administrator account is created as pending until SpeakOut verifies the school.

After approval

  • Your institution receives a unique school code.
  • The primary administrator activates the School Portal.
  • Students join with the code plus their registration/matric number.
  • Your administrator verifies student membership.
  • Verified learners are connected to your institution for programmes and reporting.

Verification & Approval

School membership is verified, not assumed.

A partner-school code identifies the institution but does not prove student status. School-linked students provide their official registration or matric number and remain pending until the school's administrator approves them.

While verification is pending, public SpeakOut resources can remain available; institution-only access is activated after approval.
